Models and safeguards

The main models come from Anthropic and OpenAI. Claude powers most writing features, with OpenAI in others and for generated images.

The provider, what leaves your device, what is remembered and the safeguards in place differ from app to app. Most artwork is made ahead of time and ships with the app.
