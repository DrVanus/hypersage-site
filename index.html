Our intelligence layer

Anyone can call the same models. The layer around them is ours.

Where a product uses AI, the models come from Anthropic and OpenAI: Claude in most of the products that write to you, OpenAI in others and for any image a product generates as you use it. What we build is the layer around them — what to ask, what the product already knows about your situation, and what it checks before you see an answer.

How a response is formed

  1. Your context — goals, preferences, conditions, and boundaries you provide within the product.
  2. Recent activity — relevant diary entries, symptoms, check-ins, conversations, and in-product activity.
  3. Product intelligence — the current task, domain knowledge, live data, tools, rules, and safeguards.
  4. The best possible answer — an answer, brief, recipe, story beat, or next action, grounded in your context and checked before you see it.

The studio

Product first

Start with the job

We define what a product needs to know, check, and help someone do before choosing the model, tools, or interface.

Shared engineering

Build the hard parts once

Voice, safety checks, and the work behind a good AI answer carry over between products. Each product adapts them to its own domain.

Kept running

The servers are ours too

We build and run our own backends — deploying them, keeping them up, and paying for them is part of shipping a product.
