Product intelligence

The intelligence is in how it all works together.

We build the context, tools, and checks around each product’s AI. That work turns context into portfolio explanations, adapted recipes, visit briefs, branching stories, and practical next steps.

How a response is formed

  1. Your context — goals, preferences, conditions, and boundaries you provide within the product.
  2. Recent activity — relevant diary entries, symptoms, check-ins, conversations, and in-product activity.
  3. Product intelligence — the current task, domain knowledge, live data, tools, rules, and safeguards.
  4. A useful result — an answer, visit brief, recipe, story beat, or next action shaped to the task. Available actions and checks vary by product and feature.
