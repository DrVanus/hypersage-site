What we do

Human vision, AI-powered execution.

People set the direction, make the hard calls, and hold the standard — AI handles the volume so craft doesn't get sacrificed for speed.

Model-first product design

The model is the starting point, not the product. We build the surface by hand — custom illustration, voice, motion, and copy — so nothing ships on generic defaults.

AI-augmented development

We run a shared harness of task-specific tooling — for art, for verification, for release — so the same standard applies across every product. Every product decision is made by a person.

Applied research

We run live experiments in autonomous trading and market intelligence — quantitative systems that read markets and act, engineered with the same rigor as our consumer apps.

Ship, then sharpen

Working builds in real hands teach more than any mockup. We ship, listen, and keep sharpening until it feels right.

The model stack

Built on Claude and GPT — and specific about which does what.

We don't train models. We buy the best ones and spend our effort on everything around them: what the model is told, what it is trusted with, and what happens when it is wrong. Two of our products contain no language model at all.

Anthropic

Claude writes the companions

Every word Rowan, Alder, Quietoak, Mythkin and Wingmate say to you is written by Claude. Free tiers run Claude Haiku; subscribers move to Claude Sonnet — the same companion, thinking harder.

claude-sonnet-5claude-haiku-4-55 products
Safety

A second model reads every message first

In Rowan, Alder and Quietoak, screening is a separate call from the one writing the reply: a deterministic word-and-pattern scan that needs no network, plus a cheaper Claude model for the cases the scan can't settle. The companion cannot overrule its verdict, and the region your phone reports decides which crisis lines you're shown.

Independent classifierZero-network first passRegion-aware
Disclosure

The build fails if we misdescribe it

Alder talks to exactly one AI vendor, and a committed check refuses to pass if that stops being true — if the copy names a provider the deployment isn't configured for, or names one and not the other, the build breaks. A promise nobody can quietly drift away from.

OpenAI

OpenAI paints every picture

All the AI art across the portfolio is made with OpenAI's image models — gpt-image-1 and gpt-image-2 — and they are the only image models a user of ours ever reaches. Plenty of what you see isn't AI at all: Alder's growth chips and Rowan's medication glyphs are drawn in code.

Voice

OpenAI speaks, where an app speaks

Quietoak's six companions, Saffra, StoryVault and Nightshelf's premium narration are voiced by OpenAI. Not everything is: Rowan reads replies aloud in your phone's own voice, so the words aren't sent anywhere to be spoken, and Wingmate's dictation never leaves the device. Alder and Mythkin don't speak at all.

And where GPT reasons

Two products lead with OpenAI

Saffra's chef runs on GPT-4o — it reads your pantry, invents the recipe and photographs it in the same turn. CryptoSage's assistant runs on GPT-4.1, and its price forecasts are generated on our servers by DeepSeek's models, not on your phone.

gpt-4ogpt-4.1-miniDeepSeek, server-side

And two products call no model at all. Hexhunter runs entirely on the device — its only network use is Apple's own StoreKit and Game Center. Waddleton never sends anything to an AI: it's a children's game, so its penguin chat is a hand-written phrase library behind a word filter, deterministic on purpose.

In-app companions

The difference isn't the model. It's what the model was handed.

A chatbot bolted onto an app knows nothing about the app. Ours are given the record the app has been keeping — and in Rowan, Alder and Quietoak there is no account, so that record lives on your phone and rides up with the message.

Context

It has read the last seven days

Three apps, one shape. Alder arrives knowing your last seven check-ins — the craving you rated, whether you used, whether you were hungry, angry, lonely or tired. Quietoak has your seven diary cards with the emotions you rated on them. Rowan has your last seven symptom entries at the severity you set, next to your conditions, medications and allergies.

Boundaries

And what it is deliberately not given

Rowan gets your medication names and schedules and pointedly not the doses. Wingmate's coach sees the one connection you have open and nothing from any other. CryptoSage sends holdings only while you leave that switch on. Mythkin shows you every fact it has kept, sorted, and lets you pin, edit or delete any of them.

The art pipeline

Nothing here is stock art.

Nine of our products generate their art through one shared in-house core, from 127 job specifications describing 645 distinct named assets. It is why a recovery app, a bedtime reader and a penguin island don't look like the same template with the hue rotated.

The core

One core, and it counts the money

One key contract, one retry policy, one 18-row price table — and a batch with no spend ceiling refuses to run rather than running uncapped. Retries are priced as money, because a render that times out after the image was made still bills. The key is attached to nothing but OpenAI, over TLS.

The craft

Judged at the size it is actually drawn

Art is reviewed on a contact sheet at true draw size on a dark field, because a sprite is judged against its neighbours and not at 100% zoom of the master — and a 1300px master is refused for a 120px draw. A repair composites only the masked region back and proves nothing else moved. A person picks the set.

About

A small team with big tools.

Hypersage is an independent studio building an eclectic family of consumer apps. People direct the work and set the standard; AI carries the volume so nothing ships half-finished.

Each product is its own brand, with its own voice and visual identity. What unites them is a single design rule: the AI is transparent about what it is.

We're building toward launch, making the work public as we go. If any of this resonates, say hello.

  • Craft over defaults Custom illustration, voice, and motion in every app. If it looks generic, it isn't done.
  • Honest by design No fake experts, no dark patterns, no gating what matters. The AI is clear about what it is and what it can't do.
  • Ship, then sharpen Real use teaches faster than any mockup. We launch, listen, and improve continuously.
  • Leverage through AI AI handles the heavy lifting so the team can focus on taste, strategy, and the decisions that count.
