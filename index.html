Our intelligence layer

We build on Claude and OpenAI. The layer around them is ours.

Where a product uses AI, it runs on models from Anthropic and OpenAI: Claude is the primary model in most of the products that write to you, OpenAI leads in others and makes every image a product generates while you use it. Any studio can call those same models. What we build is the layer around them — the part that decides what to ask, what the product already knows about your situation, and what it checks before you see an answer.

  1. Your context

    Goals, preferences, conditions, and boundaries you provide within the product.

  2. Recent activity

    Relevant diary entries, symptoms, check-ins, conversations, and in-product activity.

  3. Product intelligence

    Current task, domain knowledge, live data, tools, rules, and safeguards.

  4. A useful result

    An answer, brief, recipe, story beat, or next action shaped for the task.

Not every product uses AI. Where one does, the provider, data path, memory, on-device versus cloud processing, and safety controls vary by product and feature — and most artwork ships with the app rather than being generated while you use it.
