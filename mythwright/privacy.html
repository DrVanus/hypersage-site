Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

Mythwright ("the app", "we", "us") is a collectible card game for iOS. We believe games should be fun without harvesting your life. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect.

Information we collect

Mythwright is designed to be played with as little personal data as possible.

  • Gameplay data — your collection, decks, progress, and match results are stored to run the game. Where you use online features, this may sync to our servers tied to an anonymous account identifier.
  • AI Card Forge prompts — text you enter to generate a card is sent to our AI provider to produce the card, and is not used to identify you.
  • Diagnostics — anonymized crash and performance data to help us fix bugs. This contains no card content or personal identifiers.

We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, or browsing history.

How we use information

  • To run matches, save your collection, and sync progress across your devices.
  • To generate cards you request through the AI Card Forge.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve balance and performance.

Third-party services

We use Apple services for the App Store and, where enabled, iCloud sync. AI card generation is processed by a third-party model provider. These providers process data only to deliver their function and under their own terms.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share personal information with advertisers.

Children's privacy

Mythwright is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can play offline, reset your local collection from within the app, or request deletion of any server-synced data by contacting us.
