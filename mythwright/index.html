A Cast of 256

Most cards are legends from classic literature and myth — Dracula, Sherlock, Athena, Alice, Ahab, Beowulf. Each ability is drawn from its source: Hyde transforms at end of turn, Cheshire vanishes to another lane.

AI Card Forge

Describe a character in one line — "a one-eyed lighthouse keeper who commands ravens" — and the Forge paints the art, balances the stats, and writes the ability. It joins your collection, yours to play. Forging is part of Mythwright Pro: it needs an account, a subscription, and a daily allowance of forges.

Campaign, Ranked & Practice

Fight literary boss decks for legendary unlocks, climb the seasonal cube ladder from Bronze to Infinite, or test new decks against the AI. Daily missions keep the collection growing.

Win the war across three lanes

Six turns. Both sides stage face-down, then reveal at once. Best two of three lanes takes the match.

Three lanes, one cube at stake — locations like Castle Dracula and the Styx twist the rules.

I

Stage

Commit cards face-down to any lane. Locations twist the rules — Castle Dracula buffs Gothic, the Styx destroys its lane on turn six.

II

Reveal

Both sides flip at once. On-reveal abilities cascade, powers stack, and lane scores swing.

III

Wager

Every match starts at one cube. Confident? Wager to double the stakes. Outmatched? Yield and cut your losses.
