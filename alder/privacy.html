Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-31

Alder has not launched. This page describes how Alder is built to work, and separates clearly what is true today from what will be true when conversations go live. It will be reviewed by counsel and updated before Alder is offered to anyone, and the date above will change when it is.

A recovery record is among the most sensitive things a person can keep on a phone — the kind of information that, in the wrong hands, costs people jobs, custody, and relationships. So this policy is written to be checked rather than trusted, and it deliberately avoids claiming protections Alder does not have.

Who we are

Alder is an iOS app from HyperSage AI Labs, built by an independent developer. There is no account and no login — no name, no email, no password. The pre-launch build assigns no identifier of any kind, because nothing is sent anywhere; when conversations go live, each install is designed to be identified by a random identifier stored on your device and nothing else. Contact: hi@hypersage.ai.

What we will never claim

Plenty of apps in this category imply a legal confidentiality they don't have. We won't:

  • Alder is not HIPAA-covered, and we make no HIPAA claim. HIPAA governs healthcare providers, health plans, and their business associates — a consumer app is none of those.
  • Alder is not covered by 42 CFR Part 2, the federal rule that protects records held by substance-use treatment programs. Alder is not such a program.
  • What we can say is what's actually true: no account, kept on your device, minimal retention, and deletable by you at any time.

What is true today

Alder is a pre-launch build. As it stands right now, there is no Alder server, and nothing you enter leaves your device. Your targets, clocks, check-ins, urge logs, debriefs, and settings are stored locally in the app. The only outbound links are ones you tap yourself — a phone number, or a public resource such as findtreatment.gov.

What will happen when conversations go live

Talking to Alder requires an AI model, and that means messages leave the device. When that ships, here is exactly how it will work — and you will be shown this and asked to agree before a single message is sent:

  • Your messages will be sent to our backend and forwarded to Anthropic (Claude), with OpenAI used only if Anthropic is unavailable. Both providers will be named to you, per data path, before you consent.
  • Both providers' current API terms say business/API inputs are not used to train their models, and not training on your words is a requirement we will hold any provider to before a single message is routed. We have no signed provider agreement yet, so treat this as the standard we are building to, not a guarantee already in force — and if we cannot get it in writing, this page will say so.
  • Conversations will be retained for at most 30 days, then deleted.
  • Your recovery record itself — the clocks, check-ins, urge logs, and debriefs — is designed to stay on your device, not on our server.
  • Everything will be tied to a random device identifier and nothing else. We will hold no name, email, or login that could identify you.

If any of that changes before launch, this page changes first.

What we do not collect — now or later

  • Your name, email, or login credentials. The app has no account.
  • Your location, contacts, photos, or calendar.
  • Any advertising identifier. There are no ad SDKs and no third-party marketing or analytics SDKs in Alder, and there never will be.
  • Data about you from anywhere outside Alder.

We do not sell or share your data. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to insurers, not to anyone.

Discretion on your device

Privacy here isn't only about servers — often the real risk is someone else picking up your phone. Two things are planned for that, and neither works yet. Please do not rely on either one today:

  • A discreet alternate app icon. The artwork is drawn and bundled with the iOS build, but there is no setting to switch it on, so the home screen currently shows the Alder icon and the name "Alder".
  • Neutral-language notifications. Alder sends no notifications at all today — the feature is unbuilt, so no notification copy exists yet. The intent is that nothing on a lock screen ever names recovery or a substance, and that every nudge is opt-in.

If your safety depends on nobody seeing this app, use your phone's own controls — a passcode or Face ID on the device, and hiding or removing the app — and check back here when these ship.

Third parties

Once conversations are live, the following are involved. Until then, none of them receive anything from Alder.

  • Anthropic — primary AI provider for Alder's replies. anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • OpenAI — failover AI provider. openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Apple App Store — app distribution. apple.com/legal/privacy

Your choices & rights

  • Export: you can export your full record from inside the app and keep it wherever you like.
  • Delete: you can delete everything from inside the app. Deleting the app itself removes all on-device data. Once conversations are live, deletion will also purge anything stored for your device identifier.
  • EU/UK (GDPR) & California (CCPA/CPRA): you may request access to, or deletion of, data tied to your device identifier, and you can withdraw consent at any time. We do not sell or share your personal information, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. Our legal bases (GDPR) are your consent for AI processing and our legitimate interest in operating and securing the service.
  • Consumer health data laws — including Washington's My Health My Data Act — treat information about substance use as a protected category with its own consent requirements. Alder is built to that standard: nothing is collected until you have affirmatively agreed, having first been shown which providers are involved.
  • Email hi@hypersage.ai with any request.

Children

Alder is intended for adults 18 and older, is age-gated at first launch, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used the app, contact hi@hypersage.ai.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and post the new version here. Material changes will be noted in the App Store update notes.
