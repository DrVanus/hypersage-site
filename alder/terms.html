Last updated: 2026-07-31

These terms govern your use of the Alder app. By using Alder, you agree to them. Please read the sections on what Alder is not, and on emergencies — those are the ones that matter.

What Alder is — and is not

Alder is a self-help wellness app that helps you track sober time, log how you're doing, ride out difficult moments, and find your way toward real support. It is not therapy, not counseling, not treatment, not clinical care, and not a diagnosis. Using it does not create a professional relationship of any kind, with anyone. It is not a substitute for professional care, for medication, or for the people in your life.

Alder is an AI

Alder is an artificial-intelligence companion — not a therapist or a doctor, and not a stand-in for the people who support your recovery, and not a person. It is disclosed as AI in the app: a standing label sits at the top of the chat screen, and it re-states what it is as time goes on. Like any AI, it can be wrong, and it can be wrong in a way that sounds certain. Do not rely on it for medical, legal, financial, or emergency decisions.

What Alder will not do

These are enforced product limits, not aspirations:

  • It gives no detox, tapering, or dosing guidance — ever, for any substance. Stopping some substances abruptly can be dangerous; that is a conversation for a medical professional.
  • It does not diagnose you, score how severe your situation is, or predict whether you'll relapse.
  • It does not tell you that you are cured.
  • It does not encourage, permit, or glamorize use, and it will not help you obtain or conceal anything.
  • It does not shame you for a lapse.
  • It does not discourage professional care, medication, or meetings. Medication for opioid or alcohol use — buprenorphine, methadone, naltrexone — is recovery, and Alder treats it that way.

Emergencies

Alder is not an emergency service and is not monitored by a person. Nobody reads your messages as you send them, and Alder cannot contact anyone on your behalf. If you or someone else is in danger, call 911. In the US you can also call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), call 1-800-484-3731 (Never Use Alone), or reach the SAMHSA National Helpline at 1-800-662-4357, free and confidential, 24/7. For gambling, 1-800-GAMBLER. These resources are always reachable in the app, and are never metered or placed behind a payment.

Eligibility

Alder is intended for adults 18 and older and is age-gated at first launch. By using it you confirm that you are at least 18. It is offered in the United States first; the crisis resources listed in the app are US resources.

Your content

What is never behind a payment. Your sober clock, check-ins and the crisis kit are free forever.
