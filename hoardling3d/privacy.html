Hoardling 3D

Privacy

Last updated 13 September 2026

The short version

Hoardling 3D has no accounts, no sign-up form, and no name or email to give us. Your progress is stored on your device, not on our servers. We run no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. Hoardling 3D has no leaderboard, so nothing about your play leaves your device.

What this build is

Hoardling 3D is an experimental, in-progress fork of Hoardling that swaps the finished game's painted 2D scene for a real-time 3D one. The rules, the raiders, and your progress are the game's own — only the rendering differs, and it's rougher than the main build by design (see the game's own page for the version we consider finished). Everything below applies to this 3D build specifically.

What stays on your device

Everything about your run of the game — campaign stars, your best Daily Siege wave, which Forge upgrades you've bought, which trial badges you've earned, which raiders you've met, and your sound preference — is written to your browser's local storage. Deleting the site's data (or uninstalling the app if you added it to your Home Screen) deletes it. There is no server copy and no backup, so we cannot restore your progress if you clear your browser or switch devices.

No leaderboard

Hoardling 3D has no leaderboard and makes no connection to one: it creates no session, and sends no score or any other detail of your play anywhere.

Before 13 September 2026 it did. Opening the game issued your browser an anonymous session from our database provider, Supabase (no email, password or personal detail), and Daily Siege runs were posted automatically to a public board of this build's own, under a generated WICK-XXXX name. That has stopped. A session created then may still be in your browser's storage (clear this site's data to remove it). Hoardling 3D no longer uses it; the main Hoardling game, which shares storage with this page because both are served from hypersage.ai, uses it only if you tell that game to post your scores.

Hoardling 3D and the main Hoardling game share your sound setting if you play both in the same browser. Your campaign progress is kept completely separate: playing one never touches your saved stars or Daily best in the other.

What we never collect

  • No name, email address, or phone number — there is no sign-up anywhere in the app.
  • No advertising identifiers, no ad SDK, no cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service is loaded by this build.
  • No camera, microphone, contacts, or location access is requested — Hoardling 3D is a WebGL game with touch/tap controls only.

What we can't remove

A Daily Siege score posted by this build before 13 September 2026 is a row on a public leaderboard, tagged only with its generated WICK-XXXX name and a wave number, not your device, browser, or IP address. Because the tag carries nothing that identifies you, there is currently no way for us to find and delete one score out of the board on request; the whole board can be reset. If that changes, this page will say so on the day it ships.

Purchases and ads

Hoardling 3D has no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no advertising in this build. If that ever changes, this page and the app's own screens will be updated on the same day, before the change ships.

Children

Hoardling 3D shares its rules and content with the main game, built to a kid-safe standard (target age 9+): comic dark-fantasy, no blood or gore, and raider "defeats" play as slapstick, not violence. The app collects no personal information from anyone, of any age, and has no chat, no messaging, no leaderboard, and no way for one player to contact another.

Changes

If what the app does changes, this page changes on the same day. The date at the top is the honest one.
