Common questions

Is this the real Hoardling?

It's the same game underneath — same rules, same raiders, same progression — rendered in real-time 3D instead of painted 2D. It's an in-progress experiment, rougher than the finished build. Play Hoardling for the version we consider finished.

I lost my progress.

Campaign stars, Forge, trials, and your Daily best are all stored in your browser's local storage, on that one device, separately from the main Hoardling game's save. Clearing your browser data, using a different browser, or switching phones starts a fresh save — there is no account to sign back into and no cloud backup.

Is Hoardling 3D free? Is there anything to buy?

Yes, it's free, and there is nothing to buy in this build — no purchases, no subscriptions, no ads.

Why don't I have a leaderboard name?

You do — it's generated automatically the first time you post a Daily Siege score, in the shape WICK-XXXX. There's no name field to fill in. This build's board is separate from the main Hoardling game's.

Something looks visually broken (a stretched raider, a black gap, a camera glitch).

That's expected more often here than in the main game — this build is an active 3D rendering experiment. Tell us what you saw, on what device, and roughly where on the map, and we'll take a look.

Report a bug

Tell us what happened, what you expected instead, and (if you can) which map, wave, or menu you were in at support@hypersage.ai.
