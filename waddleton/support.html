Quick help

My penguin is gone

The current iPhone app does not offer sign-in or cross-device save recovery. Your save depends on the app or browser where it began; a phone and a browser are separate penguins. Clearing data, reinstalling, or changing devices can lose that save. Restore Purchases can check active Club membership and unfinished transactions, but cannot recover a lost penguin or completed Goldfish purchases. Please contact support before clearing data if something looks wrong.

The island is empty

The island has scripted residents as well as real players. A resident's player card identifies them. You see real players who are in the same area while online services are connected; Town Square is a good place to meet. If the connection drops, reopen the app or refresh the browser. An empty area does not mean your save is lost.

Someone is bothering me

Tap their penguin and use mute or block — both work immediately, and blocking hides them from you entirely. You can also report them. Please don't reply, and never share personal details with anyone in the game.

My score isn't on the leaderboard

Shared leaderboards are available for sixteen of the seventeen games while online services are connected; the exception is Lazy Floe, which has no score to beat. A score is only posted when you beat your own personal best, so an ordinary run won't appear, and an empty board may mean no scores are available or the online service is disconnected. Teens and adults appear under the display name they chose, which everyone on that board can see. Players in the under-13 band appear under a penguin name the game generates instead, so a child's row will not match the name over their penguin — that is deliberate, and the score still counts. If a score you're sure about never appears, email us and say which game.

Is there an app?

A mobile app is on the way. For now Waddleton runs in your phone or tablet browser, and "Add to Home Screen" gives you an icon that opens it full-screen like an app.

Found a bug?

Email support@hypersage.ai with what happened, which area or game you were in, and what device and browser you were using. Screenshots are extremely helpful.

Delete your penguin

In the iPhone app, open profile → Your Account → Delete account and follow the confirmation. Keep the app open until it confirms deletion; if interrupted, use the retry action. Clearing app data alone does not remove the server account. For browser versions without this control, or if you cannot access the penguin, email support. We may need to verify ownership.

Deletion removes the current penguin's account and progress. It does not cancel Club or erase Apple's purchase records. Read the privacy policy for deletion scope and limited retention.

Refunds and purchases

Waddleton's games and shared island are free, with no ads. The browser version has no purchases. The iPhone app being prepared for release offers optional Goldfish packs and Waddleton Club for cosmetics and membership bonuses; purchases do not improve mini-game performance or leaderboard scores.

For a missing purchase, reopen the app or use Restore Purchases in the Goldfish screen with the Apple account that made the purchase. This checks active subscriptions and unfinished transactions. It does not restore completed consumable purchases, spent Goldfish, or a lost or deleted save. If delivery still fails, email support before clearing data.

Apple processes payments. You can request a refund from Apple; eligibility depends on Apple's policies and applicable law. To stop Club renewing, open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Waddleton Club and cancel. See Apple's cancellation instructions. Deleting the app or penguin does not cancel it.

For parents

Waddleton asks for birth month and year to choose an age band. The month and year are not saved; the band is saved locally and included in the online profile. Under-13 players get generated names and preset chat phrases; incoming free text is filtered from their view. Their guest identifier, progress, and other profile data are still processed as described in the privacy policy.

The iPhone app uses grown-up checks before purchases and selected external actions. These complement Apple's purchase controls, such as Ask to Buy, but are not identity verification. Parents can enable Apple's controls in their family settings.

The island includes scripted residents and other people. Tap a player to mute or block them immediately, and report inappropriate behavior. Reports are recorded, but there is no full-time team watching live chat. Do not share personal information; contact support@hypersage.ai with concerns.

For press & creators

Videos, streams, and screenshots of your own play are welcome and don't need permission. If you'd like art assets or want to talk about the game, email support@hypersage.ai.
