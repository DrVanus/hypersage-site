Quick help

My penguin is gone

Your penguin is saved in the browser you played in. If you cleared your browsing data, used a private window, or switched to a different device or browser, you'll get a new penguin — and unfortunately there's no way for us to bring the old one back, because there's no account attached to it. It's the sharpest edge in the game, and giving penguins a way to travel with you is what we most want to add next.

The island is empty

You'll only see the players who are in the same area as you at that moment. Try the Town Square, which is where most penguins end up. If you can see your own penguin but nobody else ever appears, refresh the page — the connection may have dropped.

Someone is bothering me

Tap their penguin and use mute or block — both work immediately, and blocking hides them from you entirely. You can also report them. Please don't reply, and never share personal details with anyone in the game.

My score isn't on the leaderboard

Shared leaderboards are live for most games. A score is only posted when you beat your own personal best, so an ordinary run won't appear. Scores are posted under your display name, which everyone on that board can see. If a board looks empty, it usually is — most are new. A few games don't have a shared board yet and show your personal best only; if a score you're sure about never appears, email us and say which game.

Is there an app?

A mobile app is on the way. For now Waddleton runs in your phone or tablet browser, and "Add to Home Screen" gives you an icon that opens it full-screen like an app.

Found a bug?

Email support@hypersage.ai with what happened, which area or game you were in, and what device and browser you were using. Screenshots are extremely helpful.

Refunds and purchases

Waddleton is free to play and has no ads. In the mobile app you can optionally buy Goldfish, a premium currency, or subscribe to the Waddleton Club. Everything either one buys is decorative — nothing you can buy makes a penguin better at a mini-game or moves you up a leaderboard. The browser version has no purchases at all.

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from, not by us: use Apple's Report a Problem page or Google Play's order history. We can't issue or reverse a charge, because we never take the payment.

Bought something and it didn't arrive? Reopen the app — it re-checks the store on every launch and credits anything outstanding automatically. If it still hasn't appeared, tap Restore purchases on the Goldfish screen, then email us if it's still missing.

Cancelling the Club is done in your App Store or Google Play account settings, not in the game. Cancelling stops the next payment; it doesn't refund the period you're in.

For parents

Waddleton never asks a child for a name, email, age, or address — a guest penguin needs nothing at all, and signing in is optional. The mobile app does have optional purchases for decorative items; they go through the App Store or Google Play, so your existing controls — Ask to Buy, Family Sharing, and password prompts — apply, and we'd encourage turning them on. It is a live multiplayer game, so your child can see and be seen by other players, and the display name they pick is shown to everyone and on leaderboards. There's a quick-chat list of safe pre-written phrases for players who'd rather not type freely, and mute and block work instantly. Reports are recorded, but there's no full-time moderation team reading them in real time — so mute and block are the tools to rely on. Full detail is in the privacy policy, and we're happy to answer questions directly.

For press & creators
