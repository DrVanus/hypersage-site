Quick help

My penguin is gone

Your penguin is saved in the browser you played in. If you cleared your browsing data, used a private window, or switched to a different device or browser, you'll get a new penguin — and unfortunately there's no way for us to bring the old one back, because there's no account attached to it. It's the sharpest edge in the game, and giving penguins a way to travel with you is what we most want to add next.

The island is empty

You'll only see the players who are in the same area as you at that moment. Try the Town Square, which is where most penguins end up. If you can see your own penguin but nobody else ever appears, refresh the page — the connection may have dropped.

Someone is bothering me

Tap their penguin and use mute or block — both work immediately, and blocking hides them from you entirely. You can also report them. Please don't reply, and never share personal details with anyone in the game.

My score isn't on the leaderboard

Global leaderboards aren't running yet, so no score has ever been posted to a shared board. The names and scores on the LEADERBOARDS screen are generated on your own device as practice targets, not other players. Your personal best in each game is real: it's saved in the browser you played in, and it's the number to beat for now. When the shared boards go live, we'll say so here.

Is there an app?

Not yet — Waddleton isn't on the App Store or Google Play. It runs in your phone or tablet browser, and you can use "Add to Home Screen" to get an icon that opens it full-screen like an app.

Found a bug?

Email hi@hypersage.ai with what happened, which area or game you were in, and what device and browser you were using. Screenshots are extremely helpful.

Refunds and purchases

There's nothing to refund — Waddleton has no ads and no purchases, and there is no way to spend money in the game. If anything ever asks you to pay to play Waddleton, it isn't us; please tell us.

For parents

Waddleton doesn't ask for a name, email, age, or account, and there is nothing to buy. It is a live multiplayer game, so your child can see and be seen by other players. There's a quick-chat list of safe pre-written phrases for players who'd rather not type freely, and mute and block work instantly. Reports are recorded, but there's no full-time moderation team reading them in real time — so mute and block are the tools to rely on. Full detail is in the privacy policy, and we're happy to answer questions directly.

For press & creators

Videos, streams, and screenshots of your own play are welcome and don't need permission. If you'd like art assets or want to talk about the game, email hi@hypersage.ai.
