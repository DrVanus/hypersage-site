Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-31

Waddleton is a free browser game with no ads, no purchases, and no sign-up. That means there is very little to collect in the first place, and this page is short because there isn't much to tell. Everything below is what actually happens.

Who we are

Waddleton is made by HyperSage AI Labs, an independent studio. Contact: hi@hypersage.ai.

The short version

  • No account. No email, no password, no real name.
  • No ads, and no advertising networks of any kind.
  • No analytics. There is no Google Analytics, no Firebase, no tracking pixel, no third-party telemetry in the game at all.
  • We do not sell or share anything, because there is nothing to sell.
  • What we do keep is your penguin — its name, look, and progress. Scores stay on your device for now.

What we store

Your penguin

When you first play, a guest identity is created for you automatically — a random identifier, with no email address or password attached. Against that identifier we store the display name you chose (up to 20 characters) and your penguin's state: colours, clothing, items, coins, level, badges, pets, and igloo. That's what makes your penguin still be there tomorrow.

Your scores

Global leaderboards aren't running yet, so no score currently leaves your device — your personal best in each game is saved in your browser and nowhere else. If we do turn the shared boards on, we would store the board, the score, and your display name so it can be shown there, plus a short-lived run token used to check that a score came from a real play session. We will update this page before that happens.

Reports

If you report another player, we store who reported, who was reported, the category, and the note you wrote (up to 500 characters). Please be honest and please don't include personal information about yourself or anyone else in that note.

On your own device

Your browser's local storage holds your penguin's profile, your personal best in each game, and small preferences like zoom level. Clearing your browser data clears these — and because there's no account, a cleared browser means a lost penguin. There's no way for us to recover one.

What we don't store

  • Chat is not saved. What you type is relayed live to the players near you and is not written to our database. The one exception is if someone includes it in a report.
  • No email address, no password, no real name, no date of birth.
  • No location, no contacts, no photos, no microphone or camera access.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no profile of you for advertising — there are no ads.

Anti-abuse

To stop one person creating thousands of penguins, our multiplayer server briefly keeps a count of recent guest sign-ups against the network address they came from. That's a short list of timestamps, it ages out on its own, and it isn't connected to your penguin or used for anything else. New sign-ins may also show a Cloudflare Turnstile check, which is there to tell people from bots.

Who else is involved

  • Cloudflare — hosts the game and runs the multiplayer server and the bot check. cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
  • Supabase — the database holding penguins, scores, and reports. supabase.com/privacy

Both act as service providers for us. Neither is given your data for their own purposes.

Children

Waddleton is a gentle game with a friendly art style, and plenty of younger players will find it. We have deliberately built it so that it does not ask children — or anyone — for personal information: no name, no email, no age, no account. A display name is the only text we keep, and we ask that you not use your real full name.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has entered personal information into Waddleton — in a display name or a report, for example — email hi@hypersage.ai and we will delete it.

Chatting with strangers

Waddleton is a live multiplayer world, so the penguins around you are real people. You can mute or block any player, and those take effect immediately. There is a quick-chat list of safe pre-written phrases for players who'd rather not type freely. Reports are recorded, but to be straightforward with you: there is not a full-time moderation team reviewing them in real time, so mute and block are the tools that work instantly. Parents may want to sit with younger players the first time.

Your choices

  • Delete your penguin: clearing your browser's site data for waddleton.pages.dev removes it locally. To have the stored copy deleted too, email hi@hypersage.ai with your penguin's display name and we'll remove it.
  • EU/UK (GDPR) & California (CCPA/CPRA): you may request access to, or deletion of, the data tied to your penguin. We do not sell or share personal information, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and post the new version here.
