Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-02

This policy covers Waddleton in the browser and the iPhone app being prepared for release. Waddleton has no ads and no third-party analytics in the game. The games and shared island are free to play. The iPhone app offers optional Goldfish and Waddleton Club purchases through Apple.

Who we are

Waddleton is made by HyperSage AI Labs LLC. Contact us at support@hypersage.ai.

The short version

  • No sign-up required. A guest penguin uses a random account identifier; you do not need an email address, password, or real name.
  • The iPhone app does not offer sign-in or cross-device save recovery. A phone and a browser are separate penguins. Clearing app data or reinstalling can lose your save.
  • We store the profile and progress needed to run your penguin, your age band, shared scores, and reports you submit. Purchase records are part of the mobile profile; payment-card details are not.
  • Cloudflare and Supabase run the online features. Apple processes iPhone purchases. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

What we store

Your penguin

The game saves your penguin on your device. When online features are available, it also creates a guest account and stores a cloud profile tied to that account. The profile includes your display name, appearance, items, currencies, progress, badges, pets, igloo, friends, mute and block lists, and age band. Your in-game social connections include other penguins' identifiers and names, and when you added a friend. These relationships are stored with your guest account; they do not come from your phone's address book. A cloud copy of a guest profile is not a recoverable sign-in: without the original device credentials, we cannot promise to reconnect you to it.

Your age band

Before you play, Waddleton asks for your birth month and year. These values are used on the device to select child, teen, or adult; the values themselves are not saved or sent to us. The age band is saved locally and included in the cloud profile under your guest account identifier. It controls chat, display names, and grown-up checks. This is an age declaration, not identity verification.

Optional browser sign-in

If a browser version offers a configured Google sign-in, choosing it links that provider's account identifier and email address to your penguin. The game does not use that email for marketing. Availability is shown in the account screen. This option is not offered in the current iPhone app; an Apple account used for purchases does not sign you into a Waddleton save.

Your scores

When you set a personal best and online services are available, the game sends the board, score, public name, and time to the shared leaderboard, with a short-lived run token used to check the play session. Teen and adult scores use their chosen display name, visible to other players. Please do not use your real full name. Under-13 players have generated names; their leaderboard name is generated too. Personal bests and scores waiting to upload are also stored on the device.

Purchases in the iPhone app

Apple handles payment. Waddleton receives purchase identifiers and verification results, including product and transaction identifiers and subscription status or expiry. The app records delivered purchases and membership in your profile, which may be synced to Supabase, and keeps local receipt records to prevent duplicate delivery. We do not receive your payment-card number or billing details. The browser version has no purchases.

Reports and support

A report records the reporter's account identifier, the reported player's identifier, and the selected reason. It can automatically include recent chat excerpts, the room, and related context in a details field limited to 500 characters. These excerpts may include messages from you and the player being reported. Please do not put personal information in chat or reports. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you send so we can respond.

On your device

Local storage holds your profile, scores, queued uploads, and preferences. The iPhone app also keeps a native backup of the save. This helps preserve progress but is not a guarantee against loss after uninstalling, clearing data, or changing devices.

Chat and the people you meet

The island includes scripted residents and real players; the player card identifies residents. Other players can see your display name, appearance, location within the game, and messages you send. Chat is relayed live and is not written to our game database, unless someone includes it in a report. For under-13 players, the app offers only preset phrases and filters incoming free text from their view. Mute and block act immediately. Reports let us investigate problems, but there is no full-time team watching chat in real time.

Technical services

Cloudflare hosts the browser game and the multiplayer relay. Supabase supplies accounts and the database. These services process network requests needed to provide the game, including IP addresses and technical request information under their own policies. A Cloudflare Turnstile check may be used to distinguish people from bots.

To prevent signup abuse, our relay stores the request's IP address with recent guest-registration times and counts registrations within a rolling one-hour window. Scheduled cleanup removes expired timestamps and deletes the address entry once none remain. Service or scheduling delays can postpone removal beyond the exact hour. This expiry applies to our signup counters; providers' separate network and security logs follow their own retention practices. We use these counters to limit abuse, not to determine your location or advertise to you.

The game does not request access to your device's address book, precise location, photos, microphone, or camera. In-game friend relationships are stored as described above. It does not use advertising identifiers, advertising networks, or third-party analytics. Data is shared with the providers needed to run the features described above, not sold or shared for advertising.

Children and parents

Under-13 players do not get a custom-name field or free-text chat. Their profile still uses a guest identifier, generated name, and age band as described above. The iPhone app places grown-up checks before purchases and selected external actions; these complement Apple's purchase controls, such as Ask to Buy, and are not proof of a person's age. Parents can contact support@hypersage.ai about their child's data or an unsafe interaction. Please use mute or block immediately when needed.

Deleting your penguin

In the iPhone app, open your profile → Your Account → Delete account. Review the confirmation and keep the app open until deletion is confirmed. This deletes the current guest account and associated cloud profile, scores, authored reports, known reports about that identity, and online social records, then clears that penguin from the device. If the connection fails, use the retry action; clearing app data alone does not delete the server account. In a browser version without this control, contact support@hypersage.ai for help. We may need to verify that the account is yours; a display name alone is not proof.

Deleting a penguin does not cancel Waddleton Club. Manage the subscription in your Apple account's subscription settings. A deleted save, spent Goldfish, and finished consumable purchases cannot be recovered by Restore Purchases.

Retention and deletion limits

Profile data is kept while the account exists. Limited deletion records are retained to finish interrupted requests and prevent stale devices from putting deleted identities back: relay retry records for up to 30 days and hashed identity records in the database for up to one year. These are expiry periods; scheduled cleanup can run later if a service is delayed. A hash is a technical identifier, not a promise of anonymity. Completed purchase-delivery records may remain on the device to prevent the same purchase being credited again.

Deletion does not erase Apple's transaction records, other devices' offline copies, or personal information someone may have included in unrelated report text. Service backups may retain data until they expire; deleted records must not be restored into active use. Contact support if information remains in a report or you need help with a deletion request.

Your choices

You may ask us for access to or deletion of data tied to your penguin, and exercise applicable privacy rights, by emailing support@hypersage.ai. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising, and will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
