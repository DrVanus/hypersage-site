Terms of Use

Last updated: 2026-11-02

These terms cover playing Waddleton in the browser at waddleton.pages.dev, and will also cover the mobile app once it ships. By playing, you agree to them. They're short, because the game is free and there's not much to argue about.

Playing is free

Waddleton is free to play and there are no advertisements. The mini-games and island rooms are available without paying. Snowflakes are earned through play. Some optional cosmetics use Goldfish or require Club membership.

Optional purchases

When the mobile app ships, you'll be able to optionally buy Goldfish, a premium currency, and subscribe to the Waddleton Club. Both are optional and cosmetic: they buy decorative items, they do not make anyone better at a mini-game, and nothing about the leaderboards changes if you buy them. Purchases are not available in the browser version.

  • Goldfish are consumable. They are delivered to the penguin on the purchasing device. Restore Purchases does not recover completed consumable purchases, spent currency, or a lost or deleted save.
  • No real-world value. Goldfish and Snowflakes cannot be sold, cashed out, transferred between penguins, or exchanged for anything outside the game. We may adjust how they're earned and what things cost as the game changes.
  • Payment is handled by the App Store, never by us — we never see or store your payment details. Refunds are theirs to give, under their policies.
  • If a purchase doesn't arrive, reopen the app: the game checks for unfinished transactions and active subscriptions. "Restore Purchases" in the Goldfish screen checks the store on demand. If delivery still fails, contact support before clearing your save.
  • Purchases are tied to the store account that made them, and the penguin lives on the device — see "Your penguin" below. Apple's purchase controls, including Ask to Buy where available, apply. The app also uses grown-up checks. These controls do not turn a guest save into a shared or recoverable account.

Waddleton Club (auto-renewing subscription)

In the mobile app, the Waddleton Club is an auto-renewing monthly subscription. Members get a monthly Goldfish allowance, a doubled daily login bonus, and access to Club-only cosmetic items.

  • The price and renewal period are shown in the app before you buy, in your own currency, exactly as your store reports them.
  • Payment is charged to your App Store account at confirmation.
  • The subscription renews automatically at the same price unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel it any time in your App Store account settings. Cancelling stops the next charge; it does not refund the current period.
  • If your membership lapses, Club-only items you already bought stay in your wardrobe, but you stop receiving the monthly allowance and the doubled daily bonus, and you can't buy new Club-only items until you rejoin.
  • Anything you bought with earned Snowflakes is never affected by your membership.

Your penguin

Your penguin is created without sign-up. The current iPhone app has no sign-in or cross-device save recovery. A phone and a browser are separate penguins.

  • A guest save depends on its original browser or app credentials. Clearing data, uninstalling, or switching devices can lose that penguin, even if a cloud copy exists.
  • If a browser version offers a configured sign-in, its account screen explains that option. An Apple account used for purchases is not a Waddleton save account.
  • To delete the current account in the iPhone app, open profile → Your Account → Delete account. Wait for confirmation. See the privacy policy for scope and retention limits. Deletion does not cancel a subscription; cancel Club in your Apple account's subscription settings.
  • We may reset, adjust, or remove penguins, items, or scores where needed to fix bugs or deal with cheating.

How to behave on the island

Waddleton is a shared world with scripted residents and real players. Please don't:

  • Harass, bully, threaten, or be cruel to other players.
  • Post anything sexual, hateful, violent, or otherwise unsuitable for a game younger players play.
  • Share personal information — yours or anyone else's — in chat, a display name, or a report. Please don't use a real full name.
  • Pretend to be someone else, including staff.
  • Cheat, tamper with the game, submit fake scores, automate play, or attack the servers.
  • Spam, advertise, or link people somewhere else.

You can mute or block any player at any time, and report someone who's being a problem. We may remove content, reset a name, or block access for behavior that breaks these rules. Reports can include recent chat excerpts and related context, as explained in the privacy policy. They are recorded, but there is not a full-time moderation team reviewing them in real time — mute and block are immediate and are the fastest way to deal with someone.

Leaderboards

Shared leaderboards are available for sixteen of the seventeen games when online services are connected — the exception is Lazy Floe, which has no score to beat. A score is posted only when you beat your own personal best. For a teen or an adult it is posted under your display name, where every other player on that board can see it — so please don't use a real full name. For a player in the under-13 band it is posted under a penguin name the game generates, not one that player picked, and the score counts the same. We may remove any score or name that is offensive, impersonates someone, or looks fabricated, and we may reset a board. Scores are for fun: they carry no prize and no real-world value.

Age

Waddleton is designed to be suitable for a general audience. Before you play, it asks for the month and year you were born so it can put you in an age band. Those two numbers are not stored; the band is saved on the device and synced with the guest account's cloud profile. The privacy policy explains what is kept and where it goes. The band decides how chat works and what your penguin may be called. Profiles still use identifiers and the data described in the privacy policy. Everything sold is optional and decorative. If you are under the age where you can agree to terms on your own, please play with a parent or guardian's permission. Parents: please read the notes on chat and children in our privacy policy.
