Terms of Use

Last updated: 2026-07-31

These terms cover playing Waddleton at waddleton.pages.dev. By playing, you agree to them. They're short, because the game is free and there's not much to argue about.

Playing is free

Waddleton costs nothing. There are no advertisements and no in-app purchases — there is no way to spend money in the game. Snowflakes and Goldfish are in-game currency with no real-world value: they can't be bought, sold, cashed out, or transferred, and we may adjust how they're earned or what they cost as the game changes.

Your penguin

Your penguin is created automatically without an account, and lives in the browser you're playing in. That has consequences worth stating plainly:

  • Progress is tied to that browser on that device. A different device is a different penguin.
  • Clearing your browser data loses your penguin permanently, and we cannot restore it. There is no account to recover.
  • We may reset, adjust, or remove penguins, items, or scores where needed to fix bugs or deal with cheating.

How to behave on the island

Waddleton is a shared, family-friendly world. Please don't:

  • Harass, bully, threaten, or be cruel to other players.
  • Post anything sexual, hateful, violent, or otherwise unsuitable for a game younger players play.
  • Share personal information — yours or anyone else's — in chat, a display name, or a report. Please don't use a real full name.
  • Pretend to be someone else, including staff.
  • Cheat, tamper with the game, submit fake scores, automate play, or attack the servers.
  • Spam, advertise, or link people somewhere else.

You can mute or block any player at any time, and report someone who's being a problem. We may remove content, reset a name, or block access for behaviour that breaks these rules. Reports are recorded, but there is not a full-time moderation team reviewing them in real time — mute and block are immediate and are the fastest way to deal with someone.

Leaderboards

Global leaderboards are not switched on. They have never been switched on, so no score has ever been posted to a shared board and no player has ever seen one. The names and scores on the LEADERBOARDS screen are generated on your own device as practice targets — they are not other players. What is real is your personal best in each game, saved in the browser you played in. If we turn real boards on, we will say so here, and scores that look fabricated may be removed.

Age

Waddleton is designed to be suitable for a general audience. It doesn't ask anyone for personal information, and there's nothing to buy. If you are under the age where you can agree to terms on your own, please play with a parent or guardian's permission. Parents: please read the notes on chat and children in our privacy policy.
