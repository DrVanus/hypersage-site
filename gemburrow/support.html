Support

We answer email. Usually within a day or two.

Reach a human: support@hypersage.ai. Tell us your iPhone model and iOS version, and what happened just before the problem — that is nearly always enough to find it.

Common questions

I'm stuck on a career level.

Every one of the 40 career levels is clearable with the standard 55-swing pickaxe — we check that before shipping, with a bot that plays all of them. If a level is fighting you: read the cards before you dig, take rocks that are holding a wall up rather than the loose gem on top, and remember that a gem you never deliver earns nothing at all.

If you would rather have a hand, the fail screen offers a Deeper Pick for 250 coins: eight extra swings on one retry of that level. It is bought with coins you dug, never with money.

My progress is gone.

Progress lives in your phone's app storage. Deleting the app deletes it, and it does not transfer between devices — there is no account to carry it. If progress vanished without you deleting anything, please email us: include your MOLE- name from the Daily results screen if you still have it, and roughly what career level and hoard count you had.

There is no sound.

Check the ring/silent switch on the side of the phone first, then open the gear in the bottom-right of the dig screen: music and sound effects have separate mutes, so one can be off while the other plays. If the music stopped mid-shift and never came back, that is a bug and we want to hear about it — tell us what you did just before it went quiet.

My Daily Dig score isn't on the board.

Only the Daily Dig posts a score, and only your best run of that day stands — a later, worse run will not replace a better one. If you dug while offline, the score is held on your phone and sent the next time you open the game with a connection. The board resets at midnight UTC, which may be the middle of your afternoon.

Take my score off the leaderboard.

Email us the MOLE- name shown on your results screen and the day, and we will delete the row.

How do I restore my purchases?

There is nothing to restore — Gemburrow has no in-app purchases and no subscription. Coins are earned by digging, and everything the shop sells is bought with them.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. That removes the save and the random identifier with it. The only thing that could remain anywhere else is a Daily Dig score row, which we will delete on request — see above.

Is there an Android or iPad version?

Gemburrow is an iPhone game, in portrait. It is built on a stack that can reach Android, so a version there is plausible later, but nothing is promised and there is no date.

Reporting a bug

The most useful bug report tells us:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • which mode you were in — Career (and the level), Daily Dig, or Free Dig;
  • what you did immediately before, and what you expected instead;
  • a screenshot or a screen recording, if you can catch it.

Send it to support@hypersage.ai. We read every one.
