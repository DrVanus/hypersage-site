THE MOLE'S FIELD GUIDE

Every swing counts.

1

Dig from the top.

Tap a gem or rock with nothing above it. Each dig uses a swing and the pile settles into the gap. Clear what is above a buried gem to reach it.

Sparkly rocks hide gems. Bigger ones can hold more.

2

Plan around an order.

Gems go into your bag. A card pays automatically when you have its whole set. The numbers show what is in your bag out of what the card needs.

Tap a card to keep that order in focus while you dig. Its missing gems stay marked as the pile settles: solid gold rings are reachable; dashed violet rings are buried. Tap the card again to clear the focus, or another card to change your plan. The line below your bag shows how much of the set you have. The focus clears when that order leaves the line. Gem symbols in Settings add another way to match pieces.

3

Leave room in your bag.

Slots are limited. Dim, crossed-out gems are spare for the cards currently on the line. Tap a bagged gem to discard it permanently for no coins.

An ordinary gem dug into a full bag is lost. Make space before digging.

4

Choose what the dragon keeps.

When an order wants a prism, choose whether to fill it or grow your dragon's collection. You have 6 seconds. If you wait, it goes to your bag when wanted and there is room; otherwise the dragon keeps it. Unwanted prisms go straight to the hoard.

A Heartstone takes three pries. In Career it gives the dragon 12 hoard. In Free and Daily, choose 600 coins or 12 hoard; waiting sells it.

5

Finish the line.

Fill the shift's order goal before your swings run out. Each spare swing then earns 5 coins. Watch the RUSH card's own countdown when one appears.

Career banks coins on your first clear. Free banks every shift. Today's Daily banks your first finished shift; replays can improve your best.

◆

Excavations: split it or save it?

The five free sites have finite piles and one visible commission. Great gems give you a choice: split for one swing to put three loose gems into your satchel, or lift intact for two swings to preserve a specimen for Flint.

Splitting needs three empty slots. A full satchel refuses ordinary gems without spending a swing. Discarding is permanent for this attempt. Dense striped stones take two swings.

Once the commission is filled, finish for a shorter route or keep digging for specimens. Only completed digs record specimens. Your best route and discoveries are kept in the field journal; these digs do not award coins or hoard.

Nothing refills. There is no choice timer. Replay any site to discover a different route.

⌨

Play with a keyboard.

Use Tab or the arrow keys to move between controls and reachable gems. In Safari, Option+Tab may be needed. Press Enter or Space to choose. Press Escape to pause or go back.

Your shift stays paused while you read. Your system’s Reduce Motion setting quiets decorative movement. Close this guide to return to Settings.
