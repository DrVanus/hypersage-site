Billing and refunds

Where one of our apps sells a subscription, purchases are handled by Apple, not by us — request refunds through reportaproblem.apple.com, and manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Several of our products, including our game Waddleton, have nothing to buy at all.

Reporting a security issue

If you've found a security or privacy problem in one of our products, please email support@hypersage.ai with the details and give us a chance to fix it before publishing. We take these seriously and we'll credit you if you'd like.

Press & reviewers

Happy to help with assets, review builds, and background — write to support@hypersage.ai and say which product you're covering.
