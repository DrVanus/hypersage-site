The getaway

They grab the gold. Then they have to get it out.

Your treasure is sixty coins in the keep, and it is your life: lose it all and the defense ends. Build gold is a separate purse, so spending on machines never touches it. The raiders want the treasure.

  1. Grab

    They reach the pile

    A raider that makes it to the hoard takes coins: one for a Scrapling, up to twenty-five for the Hoard King. Then it turns for the cave mouth.

  2. Run

    Heavier loot, slower getaway

    The heavier the load, the slower the getaway. Your Crossbows, Mimics, Roosts and Braziers go after fleeing carriers before anything else.

  3. Home

    Kill the carrier

    Every coin a carrier is still holding when it falls flies back to the pile. Coins that reach the cave mouth are gone for good, and your stars count them.

Wick can win coins back without a kill, too. Stand him in a fleeing thief's path and he shakes its coins loose, one at a time.

How it plays

Small dragon. Clever defenses.

Pick a machine from the bar, set it down beside the road, and upgrade it as the waves get worse. Then decide where Wick himself should stand.

Build beside the road

There are no fixed tower slots. Set a machine on clear floor beside the road, wherever the fight needs it. Round stone pads take 20% off the price.

Put Wick to work

Send Wick to crew a weapon and it fires 70% faster and hits 30% harder; aboard a Bellows Post or Coin Press he strengthens its aura by 60% or its gold by 50%. Even just standing close, he overclocks the machine nearest him by 25%.

Wick's Breath

Tap BREATH to scorch every raider in Wick's reach for 26 damage, straight through armor, flyers included. It recharges in 14 seconds.

Two final forms each

Upgrade a machine twice and it asks you to choose its MAX form: one of two specializations, for good. Eight machines, sixteen final forms.

Machines that don't shoot

A Bellows Post fans the machines around it to fire faster. A Coin Press mints gold when each wave ends. Neither one fires a shot.

Mother stirs

Let the treasure fall to 15 and the keep begins to glow. Tap it and Wick's sleeping mother breathes, once a run: 60 damage to every raider on your road.

Between waves a seven-second clock runs down. Call the next wave early for a little bonus gold, or use the time to build. On a regular campaign keep the first wave waits until you're ready; trials, Daily Siege and Duels start after a short countdown.

The raiders

Ten kinds of thief.

“The Guild wants your treasure. Make them work for it.” Every raider needs a different answer. The first time each one turns up, a card tells you what it does and how to stop it.

Scrapling

Fodder with a loot sack. Everything works on him.

Filcher

The fastest thief on the road, and he grabs three coins.

Bulwark

Armor takes 5 off every direct hit. Burns, Gemsinger pulses and Breath go straight through.

Shellback

A pavise halves Crossbow and Roost bolts, until a Drillbolt breaks it for good.

Gloomwing

Flies over ground-only machines. Bring something that hits air.

Greed Hexer

Heals the raiders around him. Set an aiming machine to HEXER to hunt him.

Blinker

Teleports up the road every few seconds, unless a Gemsinger has it chilled.

Pry-Hand

Jams the machines he passes. Wick clears a jam fastest by climbing aboard.

Hogshead

Splits into two Scraplings when he falls. Bring splash.

The Hoard King

Grabs up to 25 coins at once. His war drum hurries his court, and at half health he calls more.

Ways to hold the hoard

Three keeps, one endless siege, four rivals.

Campaign

Three keeps, twenty waves each

Hold The Long Sleep, The Undergallery and The Coldroot Stair, each ending with the Hoard King. Win with treasure left, and your stars count the coins that got away.

  • Three stars if five coins or fewer escape
  • Stars unlock machines and buy lasting upgrades in the Forge, with a free respec
  • Six trials remix a keep you've already won
  • A checkpoint saves at the start of every wave
Daily Siege

An endless siege, the same for everyone

One siege per UTC day, with the same keep and the same waves for every player. There is no last wave. How many can you hold?

  • Everyone starts equal: 120 gold, all eight machines, no Forge upgrades
  • A Hoard King every tenth wave
  • Restart as often as you like; your best wave stands
  • An all-time ladder of the deepest sieges, if you say yes
Duel

One cavern, two dragons

Face a computer rival in The Split Cavern. The same raiders march down both roads for twelve waves: keep more treasure than your rival, or sack their hoard outright.

  • Four rivals, Tallow, Flint, Ember and Cinder, each with their own way of building
  • No Forge upgrades, and all eight machines on your bar
  • Your first win against each rival pays Hoard Marks
Your Cavern

New looks are earned, not bought

Spend Hoard Marks across six shelves: recolour Wick, re-mint your coins, swap the keep, repave the road. Preview any look free before you unlock it.

  • Marks come from new stars, first trial badges, first rival wins and deeper Daily Sieges

Common questions

FAQ

Where can I play Hoardling?

In your browser, at hypersage.ai/hoardling/play/. It's made for a phone held upright; on a computer it plays in a tall window framed by the cavern. It needs a connection to load. The browser keeps its own save and the iPhone app keeps a separate one, so the two don't share progress.

Does it cost anything?

No. Hoardling is free in your browser, and there is nothing to buy in this build: no purchases, no subscriptions and no ads. Every look you unlock in the Cavern is paid for with Hoard Marks you earn by playing.

How is my progress saved?

On your device. Stars, Forge upgrades, trial badges, Hoard Marks and looks are kept in your browser's storage. A campaign keep also saves a checkpoint at the start of each wave, so you can close the game and pick up from the start of that wave. Daily Siege and Duel runs are played in one sitting. There's no sign-up and no cloud copy of your progress, so clearing site data, using a private window or switching browsers starts a fresh save.

What does the leaderboard share?

Only what you agree to. The first time you open Daily Siege, the game asks whether to post your waves to a public all-time ladder. Say yes and it signs your browser in anonymously and posts your wave count under a random WICK-XXXX name; it never asks for your name or email. Say no and the game makes no leaderboard connection at all. You can stop posting from any Daily Siege results screen, and a score that has been posted can't be deleted. Campaign and Duel runs are never posted. Read the privacy policy.

How does Daily Siege work?

It's one endless siege per UTC day, the same for every player: the same keep and the same waves. Your score is how many waves you survive. Everyone starts with 120 gold and all eight machines, with no Forge upgrades, and a Hoard King arrives every tenth wave. Restart as often as you like; your best wave is the one that stands.

What are the controls?

Everything works with a tap or a click: pick a machine from the bar and tap the floor to build it, tap a machine to upgrade it or send Wick aboard, and tap the floor to move him. On a keyboard, Space calls the next wave, B breathes, 1 to 8 pick a machine, the arrow keys or WASD move Wick, Enter builds, Esc or P pauses, H opens the field guide and M turns sound on or off.

Is the art hand-painted?

No. It's AI-generated, and we'd rather say so. Wick, the machines, the raiders and the cavern come from AI image models working under one painted art direction; our own tools then cut them out, clean their edges and recolour them to sit together. The music is composed in code and rendered by our own synthesis scripts, with no samples and no licensed audio, and the sound effects are generated as you play.
