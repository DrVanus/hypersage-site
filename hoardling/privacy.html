Hoardling

Privacy

Last updated 13 September 2026

The short version

Hoardling comes two ways: as the Hoardling iPhone app, and in a web browser at hypersage.ai. This page covers both. Hoardling has no accounts, no sign-up form, and no name or email to give us. Your campaign progress is stored on your device, not on our servers. We run no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. The only thing that leaves your device is an anonymous, auto-created leaderboard identity and the wave number your Daily Siege runs post to it — never anything that identifies you. Daily Siege posting is on by default: your first Daily run is posted before you are shown the switch that turns it off. That is spelled out below.

What stays on your device

Everything about your run of the game — campaign stars, your best Daily Siege wave, which Forge upgrades you've bought, which trial badges you've earned, which raiders you've met, and your sound preference — is saved on your device. In the iPhone app the game is bundled inside the app and saves to the app's own storage on your phone; deleting the app deletes it. In a browser it is written to that browser's local storage, and deleting the site's data (or removing it if you added it to your Home Screen) deletes it. The app and the browser keep separate saves. There is no server copy and no backup, so we cannot restore your progress if you delete the app, clear your browser or switch devices.

The leaderboard identity

Hoardling has a public Daily Siege leaderboard, and posting to it is on by default. Starting a Daily Siege run issues the app or your browser an anonymous session from our database provider, Supabase — no email, password, or personal detail is asked for or collected — and when that run ends, the wave you reached is submitted to the board automatically. There is no confirmation step and no prompt.

The switch that stops posting is on the results screen at the end of a run, so your very first Daily Siege score is posted before you have ever been shown it. Once you use it, the setting is remembered on your device and no further run is posted; the same switch turns posting back on. Turning it off stops new scores and holds back any still waiting to be sent — it cannot remove a score already on the board (see below). Campaign and Duel runs are never posted; only Daily Siege is.

Scores are posted under a name we generate for you, in the fixed shape WICK-XXXX (four characters derived from the anonymous session itself). You never type a name, and the app never asks you for one. Each submission carries that name, the board, the wave number, and a one-run token the server issued when the run began — nothing else about you or your device.

In a browser only: hypersage.ai also hosts a 3D build of the game, and a browser treats every page on hypersage.ai as one site for storage purposes, so if you play both in the same browser they share your anonymous session and your sound setting, the way two tabs of one site share a login. Campaign progress is kept separate, and each posts to its own leaderboard. The iPhone app runs only its own bundled game and shares nothing with any website.

If the leaderboard can't be reached — offline, or a slow connection — your pending score is held in local storage and retried later, up to six attempts over one hour, then dropped. It never grows beyond your ten most recent runs.

What we never collect

  • No name, email address, or phone number — there is no sign-up anywhere in the app.
  • No advertising identifiers, no ad SDK, no cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service is loaded by this build.
  • No camera, microphone, contacts, or location access is requested — Hoardling is a canvas game with touch/tap controls only.

What we can't remove

A posted Daily Siege score is a row on a public leaderboard, tagged only with your generated WICK-XXXX name and a wave number — not your device, browser, or IP address by design. Because the tag carries nothing that identifies you, there is currently no way for us to find and delete one score out of the board on request; the whole board can be reset. This applies to the first Daily Siege score too — the one posted automatically before you were shown the switch. If that changes, this page will say so on the day it ships.

Purchases and ads

Hoardling has no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no advertising in this build. If that ever changes, this page and the app's own screens will be updated on the same day, before the change ships.

Children

Hoardling is a fantasy strategy game with cartoon combat. It is rated 12+ on the App Store and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age — no name, email address or contact detail — and has no chat, no messaging, and no way for one player to contact another; the leaderboard is read-only score rows, nothing else.

Changes

If what the app does changes, this page changes on the same day. The date at the top is the honest one.
