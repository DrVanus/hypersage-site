Help with Wingmate for iPhone. Last updated: November 15, 2026

Contact Us

Email support@hypersage.ai and we'll get back to you. It helps if you include your iPhone model, your iOS version, the Wingmate version from Settings → About → Version, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Please don't send passwords, payment details, or screenshots of other people's private conversations — we don't need any of them to help you.

What Wingmate Is

Wingmate is an AI dating coach, not a dating app. There is no matching, no public profiles, no messaging between users, and no way for anyone else to see you or contact you through the app. You chat with one of four coaches — Marcus, Jade, Eli, or Ava — about conversations you're having somewhere else, and you can share screenshots of those conversations to get them read back to you. Wingmate requires iOS 17 or later and is designed for iPhone.

There is no account and no sign-in. Nothing to reset, and no password to lose.

If a Coach Won't Reply

Coach replies are generated on our server and streamed back to your phone, so a reply needs a working internet connection. If a message fails or a reply stops partway:

  • Check your signal. On a weak connection the app will tell you it couldn't connect, or that the coach didn't respond in time — tap to try again.
  • Switch between Wi-Fi and cellular, and turn Airplane Mode on and off once.
  • Force-quit Wingmate (swipe up from the bottom of the screen, then swipe the app away) and reopen it. Your conversations are saved on your device and will still be there.
  • Make sure you're on the latest version of Wingmate in the App Store.
  • If it still fails after a few minutes, our server may be down. Email us and we'll tell you what's happening.

"You've Used Your Free Coaching Requests"

Every iPhone starts with 3 free coaching requests. Replies, screenshot reads, openers, Share and live keyboard replies all count, and free requests don't refill. After them, AI coaching needs Wingmate Plus, which includes up to 60 coaching messages a day; that daily allowance resets at midnight UTC. Nothing you've saved is lost while you decide. (Versions before 1.5.6 gave 3 free messages a day instead.)

Wingmate Plus

Wingmate Plus is an auto-renewing subscription, available monthly or annually, purchased through the App Store. Prices are shown in your local currency on the upgrade screen inside the app.

Plus keeps AI coaching going after your free requests. It includes up to 60 coaching messages a day with any of the four coaches: replies, screenshot coaching, openers, date planning, Share and live keyboard replies. Read-aloud and coach memory run on a separate daily allowance — one that Plus also raises, and that never spends a coaching message. Without Plus you keep coach intros, your saved openers, connections, saved replies and typing with the Wingmate keyboard. Inserting a saved reply spends no coaching request.

Restoring a Purchase

If you've paid and Plus isn't showing as active — after reinstalling, or on a new iPhone — open Settings → Wingmate Plus → Restore purchases in the app. This re-checks the Apple ID signed in on the device and re-activates the subscription if one is found.

  • Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID that bought the subscription. A subscription bought on one Apple ID cannot be restored on another.
  • If the app says it couldn't find an active subscription, check iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions to confirm the subscription is still active and hasn't expired or been cancelled.
  • Restoring needs an internet connection.

Because there's no account, your subscription follows your Apple ID, not a login. Your conversations and connections do not transfer to a new phone with it — see "Your Data" below.

Cancelling, Refunds and Billing

All payments for Wingmate are handled by Apple. We never see your card and we cannot issue a refund ourselves.

  • To cancel or change your plan: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Wingmate. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the end of the current period stops the next renewal; you keep Plus until the period ends.
  • To request a refund: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with the Apple ID that made the purchase, and choose the Wingmate charge. Apple decides refunds under their own policy.
  • For a charge you don't recognize: your App Store purchase history at reportaproblem.apple.com is the authoritative record.

If you've been charged and Plus still isn't working after a restore, email us. That part we can help with.

Screenshots and the Microphone

You can attach a conversation screenshot from your photo library, or paste one straight into the message box. The photo picker needs no permission. If the microphone button doesn't respond, check iOS Settings → Wingmate (Microphone, Speech Recognition).

Wingmate Keyboard and Screen Assist

Open Wingmate > Settings > Wingmate Keyboard. Add the keyboard in iPhone Settings with Allow Full Access, then review and allow Screen AI in Wingmate. Turn on screen assist and confirm Apple’s sharing sheet. Return to your chat. Wingmate Keyboard normally opens on Replies by itself the first time it comes up after screen assist is turned on, in whatever app that is, so the first picture is sent without another tap, once per session (not in a number field, or right after replies were saved from the app or Share). More replies or New reply asks for fresh suggestions. Try again reuses the same request. Tap a suggestion to insert it; you edit and send it yourself.

Permission alone does not start screen sharing. A session lasts up to 15 minutes and can end sooner; use Turn off in the app or keyboard to stop. Some apps and secure fields do not allow custom keyboards or screen sharing. Typing and saved replies work without Full Access or Plus. Typing alone sends nothing, but a draft visible on screen can be in a requested picture.

To withdraw the screen AI permission from the keyboard, turn screen assist off first, then open Screen AI settings in the Replies panel and tap Turn off Screen AI. While screen assist is on, the keyboard's Turn off ends the session only and the permission stays. In the app, choose Turn off Screen AI under Keyboard Screen AI in Settings → Privacy, or in Settings → Wingmate Keyboard → Privacy and options.

About You and Your Texting Voice

In 1.5.9, Settings > You separates About you (optional facts a coach can use when asked about you) from Your texting voice (examples of how you write). Saved profiles go with coaching requests to Wingmate and Anthropic. Clear in either editor asks once and removes the saved profile from the device and new requests. Existing conversations, prepared replies and retries retain their original content. Both are kept in the app's preferences on your iPhone, which iCloud and computer backups include; clearing them in Wingmate does not remove copies from backups already made. Before 1.5.9, Your texting voice is under Settings > Communication.

Your Data

This section is a plain-language summary of the Privacy Policy. If the two ever disagree, the Privacy Policy is authoritative.

  • Your saved conversations, connections and openers live on your device, with iOS file protection. Relevant content is included in coaching requests as described below; there is no server-synced conversation library.
  • When you send a message, that message, any screenshots you attached, and the details you've saved about the person you're discussing are sent over HTTPS to our server, which relays them to our AI provider (Anthropic's Claude) to write the reply. Submitted images and request text are not written to our server storage. For Share and keyboard requests, generated replies and summaries are retained for up to 30 minutes so retries do not spend another request.
  • Read-aloud sends the text of a coach's reply to ElevenLabs to generate the audio in that coach's voice. If ElevenLabs can't answer, the same text goes to OpenAI instead and the app tells you a stand-in voice is reading. Versions before 1.5.7 use OpenAI for all read-aloud.
  • There is no account. A random identifier kept in the iOS Keychain identifies your device for coaching allowance and retry checks. Subscription proof is used to verify Plus. Saved About you facts and other content sent for coaching can include names or locations, so the absence of an account does not make that content anonymous.
  • The app uses no advertising identifiers or third-party analytics, and we don't sell or rent your data.

Deleting Your Data

Saved conversations and profiles can be cleared on your device. Clearing does not recall content already sent to a provider or reset your coaching allowance. Settings → Danger Zone → Clear all conversations also clears the replies saved for the keyboard, and so does removing or archiving a connection; Settings → Wingmate Keyboard → Privacy and options → Clear saved keyboard replies removes them on their own. Most server records are temporary. The exception is the count of free coaching requests your device has used, which our server keeps with its device identifier and no expiry, because free requests don't refill. Server records and provider retention are explained in the Privacy Policy.

  • Delete all coaching conversations: in the app, Settings → Danger Zone → Clear all conversations. This is permanent and cannot be undone. Your connections list is not affected.
  • Delete a single connection (and its notes, photos and conversations) from that connection's page.
  • Delete local app content: delete Wingmate from your iPhone. Your device identifier and subscription proof can remain in the iOS Keychain, and existing device backups are not erased. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription — cancel that in iOS Settings first.

We do not keep a conversation backup that support can restore. Your own iCloud or computer backups may contain app data, including your texting voice and About you. Keep anything you need before deleting.

Reporting a Bug

Email support@hypersage.ai with:

  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
  • The steps that reproduce it, if you know them
  • iPhone model, iOS version, and the app version from Settings → About → Version
  • A screenshot of the problem, if it's something you can see — please crop out anything private

We read every message, and we'll tell you if a fix is on the way.

Advice, Safety and Limits

Coaching replies are generated by AI and are for entertainment and informational purposes. They can be wrong, and they are not a substitute for professional relationship counseling. Wingmate is intended for users 17 and older. If you're in crisis or in danger, please contact your local emergency services or a crisis line rather than an app.
