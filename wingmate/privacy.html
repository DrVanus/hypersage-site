Wingmate

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Overview

Wingmate ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Wingmate mobile application.

Information We Collect

Chat messages. When you chat with a coach, your message is sent to our server, which relays it to our AI provider (see Third-Party Processing) to generate a reply. Coach chat messages are not stored on our servers — each chat request is processed only to produce the response. Your conversation history is kept on your device. Replies prepared for Wingmate Keyboard work differently; see Prepared replies and Wingmate Keyboard below.

Screenshots. If you share conversation screenshots with your coach, they are sent to our server and relayed to our AI provider (see Third-Party Processing) to generate a response. They are not stored on our servers.

Other people in your screenshots. A conversation screenshot may include messages, names, or photos of the person you're talking to. When you choose to share one, that content is processed by our AI provider solely to generate your coaching response and is not stored on our servers or used to train models. Please only share screenshots you're comfortable processing this way, and avoid sharing content about anyone who would object.

Device identifier. We generate and store a random, anonymous device identifier to track your free-tier usage and subscription status. It is tied to your device and subscription, not to your name, email address, or phone number.

Connection data. Information you enter about people you're talking to (such as names, notes, and platforms) is stored locally on your device. When you ask a coach for advice about a specific person, the details you've entered about them are included in that request to our AI provider so the advice is relevant. We do not store this connection data on our servers. If you turn on saved context for Wingmate Keyboard, a short summary of the connection you select is included in that request and is held alongside the prepared replies for up to 30 minutes; see Prepared replies and Wingmate Keyboard below.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your real name, email address, or phone number
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or files beyond screenshots you explicitly share
  • The app does not use tracking pixels, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics
  • We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI coaching responses to your messages
  • To analyze screenshots you share for coaching purposes
  • To manage your subscription and free-tier usage limits
  • To maintain conversation continuity within coaching sessions

Third-Party Processing

To generate coaching responses, we send the content of your requests — your messages, any screenshots you share, and the details you've entered about the person you're discussing — to trusted AI providers acting as our processors:

  • Anthropic (Claude) generates the coaches' written responses and analyzes shared screenshots.
  • OpenAI generates spoken audio when you use the read-aloud (text-to-speech) feature.

These providers process your content only to return a response and do not use it to train their models under our configuration. We do not share your data with any other third parties for their own purposes.

Data Storage and Security

Your connection profiles, saved openers, and app preferences are stored locally on your device using encrypted file protection. Chat messages sent to our servers are processed over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Data Retention

Your conversation transcripts, connections, and saved openers are stored only on your device, and you can delete all conversations at any time from Settings. Coach chat messages are not retained on our servers; each chat request is processed only to generate a reply and is not saved. Replies prepared for Wingmate Keyboard are the one exception, and are described below.

Prepared replies and Wingmate Keyboard

The keyboard cannot read your screen. Wingmate Keyboard does not request Full Access, so iOS gives it no network access and no way to send anything anywhere. It cannot see your conversations, the app you are typing in, or what you type on any other keyboard. It can only display replies you have already prepared, and insert one when you tap it.

You choose what is shared. Replies are prepared from the screenshots or text you explicitly share to Wingmate, plus — only if you turn on saved context — notes you have entered about the connection you select.

Prepared replies are held for up to 30 minutes. So that you can retry a request without being charged for it twice, our server keeps the generated replies and a short summary of the context you shared for up to 30 minutes, and then deletes them. They are also stored on your device, in a space shared between Wingmate and its keyboard, until they expire or you clear them.

You can clear prepared replies at any time from Settings, and deleting a connection removes any prepared replies for that person.

Children's Privacy

Wingmate is not intended for users under the age of 17. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
