Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Overview

Wingmate ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Wingmate mobile application.

Upcoming version 1.5. The main-app AI permission, Share extension and optional live keyboard features described below apply to the upcoming version 1.5 update. Earlier versions may not include these features.

Information We Collect

Chat messages. With your AI sharing permission, your message, recent coaching history and coaching preferences are sent to our server and relayed to our AI provider (see Third-Party Processing) to generate a reply. Your saved conversation history remains on your device. Ordinary coaching requests are processed to produce a response. The optional quick-reply flow temporarily retains generated output for safe retries, as described below.

Screenshots and profile photos. Images you choose to share with a coach, including saved profile photos for a selected connection in the main app, are sent to our server and relayed to our AI provider to inform the coaching response. Raw submitted images are not stored on our servers. The Share extension receives only the images you explicitly share to it; saved profile photos from the main app are not exported to Share or the keyboard.

Live screen images. If you enable live keyboard assistance and start an iOS screen-sharing session, tapping Reply requests one fresh image of the visible screen. That image and your chosen coach and reply preferences are sent through Wingmate's service to Anthropic to suggest replies. The image can include chat messages, profile details, draft text, other apps and notifications visible at that moment. The controls and temporary storage for this optional feature are described below.

Other people in your images. A screenshot or requested screen image may include messages, names, or photos of the person you're talking to or other people. When you choose to send one, that content is processed by our AI provider solely to generate your coaching response and is processed under the providers’ applicable data-handling terms. Raw image files are not persisted in our quick-reply journal. Please only send images you're comfortable processing this way, and avoid sharing content about anyone who would object.

Device identifier. We generate and store a random device identifier to track your free-tier usage and subscription status. It is not an account or advertising identifier, but it links allowance and retry records to the same device. A verified Apple subscription transaction can similarly identify your subscription allowance. We treat this as data linked to you for App Store privacy disclosures.

Connection data. Information you enter about people you're talking to (such as names, notes, platforms and saved profile photos) is stored locally on your device. When you ask a coach for advice about a specific person and allow main-app AI sharing, the selected connection's details are included in that request. Anthropic also helps generate openers and extract coach memories from saved conversations while that permission is on. Raw submitted connection notes are not written to the quick-reply journal. A generated reply or summary may reflect that context and is subject to the temporary retention below.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require your real name, email address, or phone number; information included in messages, chosen screenshots or requested screen images is processed with that content
  • We do not access your contacts or browse your photo library; you choose which photos and screenshots to share
  • The Wingmate iOS app does not include advertising or tracking SDKs. Website visit counting is described separately below
  • We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI coaching responses to your messages
  • To analyze screenshots you share and screen images you request for coaching purposes
  • To manage your subscription and free-tier usage limits
  • To maintain conversation continuity within coaching sessions

Third-Party Processing

With your permission, we send the content needed for the requested feature through Wingmate's service to AI providers acting as our processors:

  • Anthropic (Claude) receives messages, chosen screenshots, requested screen images, recent coaching history, coaching preferences and selected connection details for the relevant coaching feature, openers and coach memories. Selected connection details in the main app can include a name, platform, notes and saved profile photos. Live keyboard requests include the requested image and coach preferences, without importing saved connection details or coaching history.
  • OpenAI receives reply text and the chosen coach voice to generate new spoken audio when you request read-aloud. Bundled introductions and cached audio play locally without a new AI request.

We do not use your messages, screenshots or requested screen images to train models. Our API providers do not use this content to train their models under our configuration; their processing is subject to the applicable API data-handling terms and our account settings. Provider retention is separate from Wingmate’s temporary reply cache. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Your AI Permissions

Starting with version 1.5, before main-app AI features send content, the AI permission screen explains the providers and information shared. Choose Allow AI sharing to enable those requests or Not now to continue browsing saved conversations and using features that work locally. In Settings > Privacy > Main app AI sharing, use Review AI permission when sharing is off or Turn off AI sharing when it is on. Turning it off blocks new main-app AI requests and cancels active connections; it cannot recall information already sent. It does not erase saved conversations or stop bundled and cached audio from playing.

The Share extension and live keyboard each ask for their own separate AI permission. Granting or withdrawing one of these three AI permissions does not change the others. None is granted by accepting onboarding terms. Keyboard Full Access and the iOS screen-sharing confirmation are separate controls as well. See the keyboard and Share controls below.

Optional Keyboard and Share Extension

Ordinary typing and saved replies. These run on your device without network requests and do not need Full Access. From Wingmate’s shared storage, the keyboard reads prepared replies, coach guidance, a short context summary and its label. Like other keyboards, it can inspect text near the cursor, selected text and the current document identifier to support typing and prevent accidental replacement. It does not send a keystroke log or that editor information as text to our service. Text visible on screen can still appear in a screen image you request with live assistance.

Optional live replies. To use live assistance, enable the keyboard's Full Access setting for network requests, review and allow the separate Keyboard AI permission, and start screen sharing through the iOS system confirmation. Allowing AI sharing or Full Access alone does not start a session or send a screen image. Each session is limited to 15 minutes and requires a new system-confirmed start to continue.

During an active session, iOS supplies screen video samples to Wingmate's extension on your device. Wingmate ignores audio, does not save a video, and does not upload a continuous video or audio stream. Only tapping Reply requests a fresh snapshot for a new AI response. Your device identifier and, if available, Apple-signed subscription proof are sent to Wingmate's service to check your existing coaching allowance. The requested image and coach preferences are relayed to Anthropic. A retry can resend the same image and request to retrieve or complete that response.

The snapshot can contain the entire visible screen, including your draft, notifications and other apps. It is not automatically limited to the chat bubbles or redacted before transmission. The coach is instructed to ignore unrelated interface content, but that content may still be in the image sent for processing. Live assistance can use only the messages and profile details visible in that image; it cannot open a person's profile, retrieve hidden or offscreen history, or identify a match with certainty. It does not import saved connections, notes, profile photos or main-app coaching history. Choosing a draft inserts text into the active field; you decide whether to edit or send it.

You can stop a session using the iOS recording indicator or request Stop screen assist in Wingmate. Choose Turn off screen AI in the keyboard or Turn off keyboard AI sharing in Wingmate's keyboard settings to withdraw that permission, cancel local requests and ask the screen-sharing session to stop. If the iOS sharing indicator remains visible, stop sharing there. Stopping, withdrawing permission or closing the keyboard cannot recall data already sent or guarantee that an accepted server request stops or its allowance is refunded.

When you share text or screenshots to Wingmate, you review that content and choose whether to send it to Anthropic for reply suggestions. Saved connection details are offered only if you enable them in Wingmate settings, and notes are included only when selected in the Share sheet. The Share extension uses the same device identity and subscription allowance as the main app. Your explicit permission to send shared content to Anthropic is remembered; you can turn it off in the Share sheet or in Wingmate Settings. This stops future preparation requests and does not undo a request you already sent.

Data Storage and Security

Your connection profiles, saved openers, and app preferences are stored locally on your device using encrypted file protection. The temporary screen-image handoff also uses iOS file protection and is excluded from device backups. Messages and images sent to our servers are processed over encrypted HTTPS connections. Temporary quick-reply results are stored on encrypted service storage.

Data Retention

Your conversation transcripts, connections, and saved openers stay on your device. You can delete conversations and clear prepared keyboard replies in Settings. Prepared replies stop being available in the keyboard after 30 minutes; local file contents are replaced when cleared or superseded.

Temporary screen images on your device. A requested frame is handed from the screen-sharing extension to the keyboard once. The handoff is usable for up to 10 seconds from the request and is cleared when consumed, cancelled or stopped. Expired content can remain in its protected local file if the processes are no longer running; cleanup removes expired leftovers when Wingmate next runs. This is not a promise of physical erasure at exactly 10 seconds.

While a live request or retry remains active, the keyboard holds the same image and request in memory so a retry cannot silently use a different screen. It releases that retained request after a usable result, an error that requires a new request, cancellation, a detected context or permission change, a session stop, or keyboard dismissal. Generated live replies stay in the keyboard's memory until cleared or dismissed and stop being available after 30 minutes. This is separate from the brief file handoff above.

For the quick-reply flow, including Share and live keyboard replies, our backend temporarily stores generated drafts, a generated context summary and explanation for up to 30 minutes of availability, so a retry can return the same result without using another coaching message. Request identifiers, hashes and allowance/result state remain for 24 hours to prevent duplicate processing. We do not persist raw submitted screenshots, screen images or request text in that journal. Records are linked to a device or subscription identifier.

Expired output is removed on startup, access, writes and a timer while the server is running. If the server is stopped, physical removal waits until it resumes. Hosting snapshots or backups may retain prior copies according to the configured hosting retention. Provider processing and retention are governed separately by the applicable provider terms. To request help with deletion of retained data, contact support@hypersage.ai. You can stop using Share or live keyboard assistance and revoke their respective AI permissions at any time; existing temporary server output continues to expire on the schedule above.

Apple’s speech-recognition service may process audio when you use voice input in the main app. Live screen assistance does not process or upload audio.
