Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Overview

Wingmate ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Wingmate mobile application.

Information We Collect

Chat messages. With your AI sharing permission, your message, recent coaching history and coaching preferences are sent to our server and relayed to our AI provider (see Third-Party Processing) to generate a reply. Your saved conversation history remains on your device. Ordinary coaching requests are processed to produce a response. The optional quick-reply flow temporarily retains generated output for safe retries, as described below.

Screenshots and profile photos. Images you choose to share with a coach, including saved profile photos for a selected connection in the main app, are sent to our server and relayed to our AI provider to inform the coaching response. Raw submitted images are not stored on our servers. The Share extension receives only the images you explicitly share to it; saved profile photos from the main app are not exported to Share or the keyboard.

Screen images from the Wingmate keyboard. If you turn on screen assist in the Wingmate keyboard, each time you tap Reply Wingmate captures one still image of your screen as it looks at that moment. That image is sent to our server and relayed to Anthropic to suggest replies. While screen assist is on, Wingmate keeps no image and sends nothing until you tap Reply. The image shows whatever is visible on your screen, which can include the other person's messages, photos and name, notification banners, the status bar and the Wingmate keyboard itself. See Optional Keyboard and Share Extension below.

Other people in your screenshots and screen images. A conversation screenshot, or a screen image you send with Reply, may include messages, names, or photos of the person you're talking to. When you choose to send one, that content is processed by our AI provider to generate your coaching response, under the protections described in Third-Party Processing. Raw screenshots and screen images are not persisted in our quick-reply journal. Please only send images you're comfortable processing this way, and avoid sharing content about anyone who would object.

Device identifier. We generate and store a random device identifier to track your free-tier usage and subscription status. It is not an account or advertising identifier, but it links allowance and retry records to the same device. A verified Apple subscription transaction can similarly identify your subscription allowance. We treat this as data linked to you for App Store privacy disclosures.

IP address. Our server receives the IP address each request comes from. We use it only to enforce daily allowances and prevent abuse: we count AI requests per IP address for the current day, and each Share or live keyboard reply request record stores the address alongside your device or subscription identifier. How long these are kept is described under Data Retention.

Connection data. Information you enter about people you're talking to (such as names, notes, platforms and saved profile photos) is stored locally on your device. When you ask a coach for advice about a specific person and allow main-app AI sharing, the selected connection's details are included in that request. Anthropic also helps generate openers and extract coach memories from saved conversations while that permission is on. Raw submitted connection notes are not written to the quick-reply journal. A generated reply or summary may reflect that context and is subject to the temporary retention below.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require your real name, email address, or phone number; information included in messages, screenshots or screen images you send is processed with that content
  • We do not access your contacts or browse your photo library; you choose which photos and screenshots to share
  • We do not record video or audio of your screen; screen assist sends one still image, and only when you tap Reply
  • The Wingmate iOS app does not include advertising or tracking SDKs. Website visit counting is described separately below
  • We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI coaching responses to your messages
  • To analyze screenshots you share, and screen images you send with Reply, for coaching purposes
  • To manage your subscription and free-tier usage limits, and to prevent abuse
  • To maintain conversation continuity within coaching sessions

Third-Party Processing

With your permission, we send the content needed for the requested feature through Wingmate's service to AI providers acting as our processors:

  • Anthropic (Claude) receives messages, chosen screenshots, recent coaching history, coaching preferences and selected connection details for coaching, openers and coach memories. Selected connection details in the main app can include a name, platform, notes and saved profile photos. When you tap Reply in the Wingmate keyboard, Anthropic receives that one screen image and your chosen coach and reply preferences, with Wingmate’s coaching instructions; your device identifier and subscription proof stay with Wingmate’s server.
  • OpenAI receives reply text and the chosen coach voice to generate new spoken audio when you request read-aloud in the main app. Bundled introductions and cached audio play locally without a new AI request. OpenAI receives nothing from the keyboard or the Share extension.

We do not use your messages, screenshots or screen images to train models. Anthropic and OpenAI each provide the same or equal protection of your data as described in this policy and as required by Apple’s App Store Review Guidelines. Each receives only the data listed above, over encrypted connections, to generate the output you requested and to monitor for abuse. Anthropic processes this data under its Commercial Terms of Service and the Data Processing Addendum incorporated into them, which prohibit training its models on it, limit its processing to providing the service on our instructions, and require appropriate security measures and a duty of confidentiality for anyone who handles it. Under those standard terms Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days, except content flagged for review under its usage policies (kept for up to 2 years) or where the law requires longer. OpenAI does not use data sent to its API to train or improve its models, and keeps abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days unless the law requires longer. This provider retention is separate from Wingmate’s temporary reply cache described below. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Your AI Permissions

Starting with version 1.5, before main-app AI features send content, the AI permission screen explains the providers and information shared. Choose Allow AI sharing to enable those requests or Not now to continue browsing saved conversations and using features that work locally. In Settings > Privacy > Main app AI sharing, use Review AI permission when sharing is off or Turn off AI sharing when it is on. Turning it off blocks new main-app AI requests and cancels active connections; it cannot recall information already sent. It does not erase saved conversations or stop bundled and cached audio from playing.

The Share extension and the keyboard’s screen assist each ask for their own separate permission. Granting or turning off any one of the three permissions (main app, Share and keyboard screen AI) does not change the others. None of them is granted by accepting onboarding terms, and keyboard Full Access and the iOS screen recording prompt are separate from all three. See the keyboard and Share controls below.

Optional Keyboard and Share Extension

Typing and saved replies. Ordinary typing in the Wingmate keyboard stays on your device and is never sent to us. From Wingmate’s shared storage the keyboard reads prepared replies, coach guidance, a short context summary and its label. Like other keyboards, it can inspect text near the cursor, selected text and the current document identifier to support typing and prevent accidental replacement; that information stays on your device and is never sent as text. Typing and saved replies work without Full Access or screen assist.

Full Access. Live coaching needs an internet connection, so the keyboard asks you to turn on Allow Full Access in iPhone Settings. With Full Access on, the keyboard contacts our server only when you tap Reply, or Try again after a request that did not finish. It does not send your keystrokes and does not read your clipboard or contacts.

Screen assist. Screen assist uses Apple’s screen broadcast feature, which you start yourself with Apple’s record button; iOS shows a recording indicator while it runs. It needs its own screen AI permission. Allowing that permission, turning on Full Access or starting the recording sends nothing by itself. While the recording runs, Wingmate does not save video, ignores audio and sends no continuous stream. Only when you tap Reply does Wingmate’s broadcast extension take one fresh frame of the screen, reduce it to at most 1280 pixels on its longest side and 1 MB, and pass it to the keyboard.

What is sent. The keyboard sends that one image, your chosen coach and reply preferences, your Wingmate device identifier and, if you subscribe, Apple’s signed subscription proof to our server. The server uses the identifier and proof only to check your coaching allowance and recognize retries, and relays the image and preferences to Anthropic, which writes up to three suggested replies. Live keyboard requests do not include saved connections, notes, profile photos, coaching history or the text around your cursor, although a draft that is visible on screen can appear in the image. If a request does not finish, Try again resends the same image and request instead of capturing a new screen.

What the image can show. The frame is your whole visible screen, not only the chat. It can include the other person’s messages, photos and name, notification banners from other apps, the status bar and the Wingmate keyboard panel. It is not cropped or redacted before it is sent; our coach is instructed to ignore notifications and unrelated interface content. Screen assist can use only what is visible in that image. It cannot open a profile you haven’t opened, retrieve hidden or earlier messages, or identify who you are talking to.

You decide what is sent to your match. Suggestions appear in the keyboard. Wingmate never sends a message for you: choosing a suggestion inserts it into the text box, where you can edit it and decide whether to send it.

Stopping and withdrawing. A screen assist session ends after 15 minutes, or sooner when you stop it in the Wingmate keyboard or from the iOS recording indicator. Continuing after that needs a new start through the iOS prompt. To withdraw the screen AI permission, tap Turn off screen AI in the keyboard, or open Wingmate and go to Settings > Wingmate Keyboard > Turn off keyboard AI sharing. Withdrawing cancels pending requests, clears suggestions shown in the keyboard and asks the recording to stop; if the iOS recording indicator is still showing, stop the recording there. You can also turn off Full Access or remove the keyboard in iPhone Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards. Stopping or withdrawing cannot recall an image that was already sent.

When you share text or screenshots to Wingmate, you review that content and choose whether to send it to Anthropic for reply suggestions. Saved connection details are offered only if you enable them in Wingmate settings, and notes are included only when selected in the Share sheet. The Share extension uses the same device identity and subscription allowance as the main app. Your explicit permission to send shared content to Anthropic is remembered; you can turn it off in the Share sheet or in Wingmate Settings. This stops future preparation requests and does not undo a request you already sent.

Data Storage and Security

Your connection profiles, saved openers, and app preferences are stored locally on your device using encrypted file protection. The temporary file that passes a screen image from the broadcast extension to the keyboard uses iOS complete file protection and is excluded from device backups. Messages and images sent to our servers travel over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Data Retention

Your conversation transcripts, connections, and saved openers stay on your device. You can delete conversations and clear prepared keyboard replies in Settings. Prepared replies stop being available in the keyboard after 30 minutes; local file contents are replaced when cleared or superseded.

Screen assist on your device. A captured frame waits in the protected handoff file only until the keyboard picks it up, and is usable for no more than 10 seconds after you tap Reply. It is removed when the keyboard uses it, when the request is cancelled or replaced, and when screen assist stops. If iOS closes Wingmate’s processes before then, the expired leftover is cleared the next time Wingmate runs. While a request is in progress or can still be retried, the keyboard holds the image in its memory; it releases it when suggestions arrive, when you tap Reply again, when you turn off screen AI or Full Access, and in any case 30 minutes after the screen was captured, once a retry could no longer return a result. Suggestions shown in the keyboard stop being available after 30 minutes.

On our server. For Share and live keyboard replies, our server does not write your screenshots, screen images or request text to storage; they are held in memory only while your replies are being prepared. Our backend stores the generated drafts, a short generated context summary and the coach’s explanation for up to 30 minutes after the request, so a retry can return the same result without using another coaching message; after that, only an expiry notice remains. A record of each request, made up of a hashed request identifier, a hash of the request content, your device or subscription identifier, the IP address the request came from and your allowance state, is kept for 24 hours to prevent duplicate processing and double charges, then deleted. Daily usage counts, kept per device or subscription and per IP address for all AI requests, reset after midnight UTC.

Expired output is removed on startup, access, writes and a timer while the server is running. If the server is stopped, physical removal waits until it resumes. Hosting snapshots or backups may retain prior copies according to the configured hosting retention. AI provider retention is described under Third-Party Processing. To request help with deletion of retained data, contact support@hypersage.ai. You can stop using Share or screen assist and withdraw either AI permission at any time; existing temporary output continues to expire on the schedule above.

Wingmate’s keyboard does not transmit keystrokes or the text around your cursor; a draft that is visible on screen can appear in a screen image you send with Reply. Screen assist ignores audio. Apple’s speech-recognition service may process audio when you use voice input in the main app.
