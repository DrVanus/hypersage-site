Wingmate

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Overview

Wingmate ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Wingmate mobile application.

Information We Collect

Chat messages. When you chat with a coach, your message is sent to our server, which relays it to our AI provider (see Third-Party Processing) to generate a reply. Your full conversation history stays on your device. Ordinary coaching requests are processed to produce a response. The optional quick-reply flow temporarily retains generated output for safe retries, as described below.

Screenshots. If you share conversation screenshots with your coach, they are sent to our server and relayed to our AI provider (see Third-Party Processing) to generate a response. They are not stored on our servers.

Other people in your screenshots. A conversation screenshot may include messages, names, or photos of the person you're talking to. When you choose to share one, that content is processed by our AI provider solely to generate your coaching response and is processed under the providers’ applicable data-handling terms. Raw screenshot files are not persisted in our quick-reply journal. Please only share screenshots you're comfortable processing this way, and avoid sharing content about anyone who would object.

Device identifier. We generate and store a random device identifier to track your free-tier usage and subscription status. It is not an account or advertising identifier, but it links allowance and retry records to the same device. A verified Apple subscription transaction can similarly identify your subscription allowance. We treat this as data linked to you for App Store privacy disclosures.

Connection data. Information you enter about people you're talking to (such as names, notes, and platforms) is stored locally on your device. When you ask a coach for advice about a specific person, the details you've entered about them are included in that request to our AI provider so the advice is relevant. Raw submitted connection notes are not written to the quick-reply journal. A generated reply or summary may reflect that context and is subject to the temporary retention below.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require your real name, email address, or phone number; information you voluntarily include in messages or screenshots is processed with that content
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or files beyond screenshots you explicitly share
  • We do not use tracking pixels, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics
  • We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI coaching responses to your messages
  • To analyze screenshots you share for coaching purposes
  • To manage your subscription and free-tier usage limits
  • To maintain conversation continuity within coaching sessions

Third-Party Processing

To generate coaching responses, we send the content of your requests — your messages, any screenshots you share, and the details you've entered about the person you're discussing — to trusted AI providers acting as our processors:

  • Anthropic (Claude) generates the coaches' written responses and analyzes shared screenshots.
  • OpenAI generates spoken audio when you use the read-aloud (text-to-speech) feature.

We do not use your messages or screenshots to train models. Our API providers do not use this content to train their models under our configuration; their processing is subject to the applicable API data-handling terms and our account settings. Provider retention is separate from Wingmate’s temporary reply cache. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Optional Keyboard and Share Extension

The Wingmate keyboard works offline and does not need Full Access. From Wingmate’s shared storage it reads prepared replies, coach guidance, a short context summary and its label. Like other keyboards, it can inspect text near the cursor, selected text and the current document identifier to support typing and prevent accidental replacement. That information stays on your device. It does not access your incoming chat bubbles, live dating profiles, screenshots, saved connection notes, or credentials. Choosing a draft inserts text into the active field; you decide whether to send it.

When you share text or screenshots to Wingmate, you review that content and choose whether to send it to Anthropic for reply suggestions. Saved connection details are offered only if you enable them in Wingmate settings, and notes are included only when selected in the Share sheet. The Share extension uses the same device identity and subscription allowance as the main app. Your explicit permission to send shared content to Anthropic is remembered; you can turn it off in the Share sheet or in Wingmate Settings. This stops future preparation requests and does not undo a request you already sent.

Data Storage and Security

Your connection profiles, saved openers, and app preferences are stored locally on your device using encrypted file protection. Chat messages sent to our servers are processed over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Data Retention

Your conversation transcripts, connections, and saved openers stay on your device. You can delete conversations and clear prepared keyboard replies in Settings. Prepared replies stop being available in the keyboard after 30 minutes; local file contents are replaced when cleared or superseded.

For the quick-reply flow, our backend temporarily stores generated drafts, a generated context summary and explanation for up to 30 minutes of availability, so a retry can return the same result without using another coaching message. Request identifiers, hashes and allowance/result state remain for 24 hours to prevent duplicate processing. We do not persist raw submitted screenshots or request text in that journal. Records are linked to a device or subscription identifier.

Expired output is removed on startup, access, writes and a timer while the server is running. If the server is stopped, physical removal waits until it resumes. Hosting snapshots or backups may retain prior copies according to the configured hosting retention. Provider processing and retention are governed separately by the applicable provider terms. To request help with deletion of retained data, contact support@hypersage.ai. You can stop using Share and revoke its AI permission at any time; existing temporary output continues to expire on the schedule above.

Wingmate’s keyboard does not transmit keystrokes or the text around your cursor. Apple’s speech-recognition service may process audio when you use voice input in the main app.

Children's Privacy

Wingmate is not intended for users under the age of 17. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
