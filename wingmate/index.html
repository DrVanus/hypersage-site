What you get

Every tool you need to stop guessing.

Real coaching, not a chatbot. Wingmate reads your conversations, knows the platform, and gives you advice that actually fits the moment.

Screenshot Analysis

Drop a screenshot of your conversation and your coach breaks it down — what's working, what's not, and exactly what to say next.

Voice Coaching

Talk it out instead of typing — dictate your message with one tap, and tap a reply to hear it read back, each coach in their own voice. Read-aloud needs a connection and has a daily allowance.

Personalized Openers

Tell your coach about her profile and get openers crafted for that person, on that platform. No copy-paste templates — every line is tailored.

Platform-Aware Coaching

Tinder is short and punchy. Hinge is prompt-based. Bumble means she messages first. Instagram is a casual DM slide — story replies, not pickup lines. And once you have her number, texting is its own game. Your coach adjusts to how each one actually works.

Date Planning

Where to go, what to do, how to keep the conversation flowing in person. Advanced logistics help so you show up with a plan, not anxiety.

Personalized for Each Connection

Track every woman you're talking to — from matching to exclusive. Your coach knows the history, the vibe, and exactly where things stand.
