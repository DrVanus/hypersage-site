What it does

Hound: an AI companion that knows your binder.

Ask what you're missing, what's worth flipping, or what a card is really worth. Hound answers with your own collection AND live market data as context, and names its sources — something no Pokémon-tracking app currently ships. Since 18 August 2026, its replies have come from a real model through our own server, not from a script.

Scanning is free. Unlimited. Forever.

No daily cap, no lifetime cap, no account to create. Card recognition runs entirely on-device against a bundled index — nothing you point the camera at leaves the device. The scanner reads English Pokémon and Magic cards, plus Japanese Pokémon from the Sun & Moon era (2017) onward; Yu-Gi-Oh!, Lorcana and Gundam are searchable from the catalogue and go into the same binder. Yu-Gi-Oh! and Lorcana carry a price; Gundam has no price feed yet, and the app says so rather than showing a number it cannot source.

Prices come with receipts.

Every price names the feed that supplied it, dated, on the card itself — and the feeds differ by game, so the app never quietly reads one market's number under another's name. For Pokémon that also means the full spread: market price, the cheapest current listing and the middle of the book. Sold-price history comes from a separate sold-listings feed, which isn't always connected and doesn't cover every card; wherever the app can't show it, it says exactly that instead of showing you a number and letting you assume.

Master-set completion, done right.

Every set you own cards in, across all five games — what you hold, what's missing, and a costed head on the gap. Owned is counted on the device and stays exact with every feed down; the totals come from each game's own catalogue and the app says when one cannot be reached. Reverse-holo printings are not counted separately yet, and the set screen says so.

No streaks. No guilt. No noise.

No manufactured urgency and no loss framing — and today, no notifications at all: this build cannot send one. When that changes, what it may interrupt you for will be stated here before it ships, not after.

Common questions

FAQ

Is scanning really free, forever?

Yes. No daily cap, no lifetime cap, no account. Scan caps are the single most common complaint in this category, so it's the one thing we committed to never do.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-in anywhere in the app. Your collection lives on your device, and CSV export is free if you want a copy of your own.

What is Hound?

Hound is the AI companion built into the iPhone app's binder — ask what you're missing from a set, or what a card is really worth, and it answers using your own collection as context, sources named. Since 18 August 2026, Hound's replies have come from Anthropic's AI model through our own server, not from pre-written examples. Everyone gets 3 messages a day; Holohound Pro raises that to 400.

What does Hound see when I ask it something?

Your message, any photo you attach, the last few messages of that conversation, and the specific card rows relevant to the question — not your whole binder. They go to our server and on to Anthropic, whose model writes the reply. No account is created; the app sends a random per-install id so the daily message allowance can be counted, and a Pro subscriber's app also sends Apple's signed record of the subscription. The privacy policy lists it in full.

Is this an official Pokémon (or Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Lorcana, Gundam) app?

No. Holohound is independent and unofficial, and is not produced, endorsed or licensed by The Pokémon Company, Nintendo, Creatures Inc., GAME FREAK, Wizards of the Coast, Konami, Disney, Ravensburger, Bandai or Sotsu/Sunrise — see the disclosure above.

How do I cancel or restore Holohound Pro?

If you subscribe, Pro belongs to your Apple ID. Cancel it in your Apple ID's subscription settings (on iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions). On a new phone, open the iPhone app's Profile → See Holohound Pro and tap Restore Purchases. The support page has the details, and we answer email.
