Holohound

Know what your cards are actually worth.

Scan a Pokémon card and get a real price with its source named — then keep the whole binder in one place. Unlimited free scanning. No account.

In development. Holohound is being built in the open and is not on the App Store yet. These pages are live because the app links to them. Want to know when it ships? Email us.

What it does

  • Scanning is free, forever. No daily cap, no lifetime cap, no account. The thing every competitor meters is the thing we never will.
  • Prices name their source. Market price, the cheapest listing and the middle of the book — from TCGplayer via TCGdex, with the date attached.
  • It says what it doesn't know. Sold-price history and graded prices aren't connected yet, so the app says exactly that instead of showing you a number labelled as something it isn't.
  • Pre-grades are a lead, not a grade. An estimate from your photo, stated as an estimate, so you can decide whether a card is worth submitting. If a card is already slabbed, we read the case instead of guessing.
  • Master-set completion. Every printing counted, what's missing, and what finishing it would cost.
  • Your data is yours. It lives on your phone, and CSV export is free and always will be.
