What it does

Built against what the category gets wrong.

Every one of these is a direct answer to a specific complaint we found reading the category's own 1- and 2-star reviews.

Hound: an AI companion that knows your binder.

Ask what you're missing, what's worth flipping, or what a card is really worth. Hound answers with your own collection AND live market data as context, and names its sources — something no Pokémon-tracking app currently ships. Live since 18 August 2026: replies come from a real model through our own server, not from a script.

Scanning is free. Unlimited. Forever.

No daily cap, no lifetime cap, no account to create. Card recognition runs entirely on your phone against a bundled index — nothing you point the camera at leaves the device. The scanner reads Pokémon and Magic cards today; Yu-Gi-Oh!, Lorcana and Gundam are searchable from the catalogue and go into the same binder. Yu-Gi-Oh! and Lorcana carry a price; Gundam has no price feed yet, and the app says so rather than showing a number it cannot source.

Prices come with receipts.

Every price names the feed that supplied it, dated, on the card itself — and the feeds differ by game, so the app never quietly reads one market's number under another's name. For Pokémon that also means the full spread: market price, the cheapest current listing and the middle of the book. Sold-price history isn't connected for any game yet, and the app says exactly that instead of showing you a number and letting you assume.

Master-set completion, done right.

Every set you own cards in, across all five games — what you hold, what's missing, and a costed head on the gap. Owned is counted on your phone and stays exact with every feed down; the totals come from each game's own catalogue and the app says when one cannot be reached. Reverse-holo printings are not counted separately yet, and the set screen says so.

No streaks. No guilt. No noise.

No manufactured urgency and no loss framing — and today, no notifications at all: this build cannot send one. When that changes, what it may interrupt you for will be stated here before it ships, not after.

Common questions

FAQ

Is scanning really free, forever?

Yes. No daily cap, no lifetime cap, no account. Scan caps are the single most common complaint in this category, so it's the one thing we committed to never do.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-in anywhere in the app. Your collection lives on your device, and CSV export is free if you want a copy of your own.

What is Hound, and can I use it now?

Hound is the AI companion built into your binder — ask what you're missing from a set, or what a card is really worth, and it answers using your own collection as context, sources named. Hound has been live since 18 August 2026: replies come from a real model through our own server, not from pre-written examples. Holohound itself is still in development and not on the App Store, so there is no build you can install yet.

What does Hound see when I ask it something?

Your message, any photo you attach, the last few messages of that conversation, and the specific card rows relevant to the question — not your whole binder. No account is created; the app sends a random per-install id so the daily message allowance can be counted. The privacy policy lists it in full.

Is this an official Pokémon (or Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Lorcana, Gundam) app?

No. Holohound is independent and unofficial, and is not produced, endorsed or licensed by The Pokémon Company, Nintendo, Creatures Inc., GAME FREAK, Wizards of the Coast, Konami, Disney, Ravensburger, Bandai or Sotsu/Sunrise — see the disclosure above.

When does it launch?

Holohound is in active development — fourteen screens are built and running on a real phone today, on real card data. There's no launch date yet. Email us to hear the moment it ships.
