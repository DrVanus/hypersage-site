What it does

Built against what the category gets wrong.

Every one of these is a direct answer to a specific complaint we found reading the category's own 1- and 2-star reviews.

Scanning is free. Unlimited. Forever.

No daily cap, no lifetime cap, no account to create. Card recognition runs entirely on your phone against a bundled index — nothing you point the camera at leaves the device.

Hound: an AI companion that knows your binder.

Ask what you're missing, what's worth flipping, or what a card is really worth. Hound answers with your own collection as context and names its sources — something no Pokémon-tracking app currently ships. Not switched on in this build yet; the FAQ below says exactly what that means today.

Prices come with receipts.

Market price, the cheapest current listing, and the middle of the book — from TCGplayer via TCGdex, dated and sourced. Sold-price history isn't connected yet, and the app says exactly that instead of showing you a number and letting you assume.

Pre-grades are a lead, not a verdict.

An estimate from your photo, with the hedge stated before the number, every time — never a flip, a count-up, or a grade the app didn't measure. Already slabbed? We read the case instead of guessing.

Master-set completion, done right.

Every printing counted — secrets and reverses included — what's missing, and what finishing it would actually cost, with a straight line to Hound to watch the prices for you.

No streaks. No guilt. No noise.

No manufactured urgency and no loss framing. Notifications are opt-in, capped under four a month, and limited to things you'd actually want to hear from a friend by text.

Common questions

FAQ

Is scanning really free, forever?

Yes. No daily cap, no lifetime cap, no account. Scan caps are the single most common complaint in this category, so it's the one thing we committed to never do.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-in anywhere in the app. Your collection lives on your device, and CSV export is free if you want a copy of your own.

What is Hound, and can I use it now?

Hound is the AI companion built into your binder — ask what you're missing from a set, what's worth flipping, or what a card is really worth, and it answers using your own collection as context, sources named. It isn't switched on in this build yet: today its replies are pre-written examples so you can see the shape of it, not a live model. This page and the privacy policy update the day that changes.

What does Hound see when I ask it something?

Once it's live: your message plus the specific card rows relevant to the question — not your whole binder, and no account is created to enable it.

How is a pre-grade different from a real grade?

A pre-grade is an estimate from your photo — a lead on whether a card is worth submitting, never a prediction of what PSA, BGS or CGC will actually return. If a card is already in a graded slab, Holohound reads the grade off the case instead of estimating.

Is this an official Pokémon app?

No. Holohound is independent and unofficial, and is not produced, endorsed or licensed by The Pokémon Company, Nintendo, Creatures Inc. or GAME FREAK — see the disclosure above.

When does it launch?

Holohound is in active development — thirteen screens are built and running on a real phone today, on real card data. There's no launch date yet. Email us to hear the moment it ships.
