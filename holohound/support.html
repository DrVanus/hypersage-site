Common questions

Is scanning limited?

No. Scanning is unlimited and free, with no daily cap and no account. Messages to Hound, the AI companion, are metered: 3 a day free, or 400 a day with Holohound Pro. The app shows how many you have used and when they reset.

Why does a price look wrong?

Prices come from a different feed for each game — TCGplayer via TCGdex for Pokémon, TCGplayer via Scryfall for Magic, YGOPRODeck for Yu-Gi-Oh!, Lorcast for Lorcana, and none at all for Gundam — and the card names its own. A market price is not the same as a sale price, so the card report also shows the cheapest current listing and the middle of the listings, so you can see the spread. Sold-price history comes from a separate sold-listings feed, which isn't always connected and doesn't cover every card; where the app can't show it for a card, it says so rather than showing you a market price labelled as a sale.

How do I get my collection out?

In the iPhone app, Profile → Export collection (CSV) saves your collection for a spreadsheet. To save a backup with your preferences, conversations and history, use Profile → Collection backup → Save backup. Both are free and need no subscription. The privacy policy lists what each backup includes.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app can remove your local collection, scan photos and install id. It does not remove exported files or backups you saved elsewhere; your device data can also be included in iPhone backups, depending on your settings. There is no Holohound account to close.

Our server keeps usage counts and subscription records described in the privacy policy. Removing records for an individual install requires its exact install id. This version does not show you that id or include it in a backup or diagnostics report, so we cannot currently identify and remove your install’s server records. Email support@hypersage.ai with questions about server-held information. Deleting the app does not cancel Holohound Pro; cancel it in your Apple ID settings (see below).

Does my collection move to a new phone?

You can move it with a Collection backup. On the old phone, open Profile → Collection backup → Save backup and save the JSON file somewhere you can access on the new phone. On the new phone, open Profile → Collection backup → Choose backup to restore. Review the preview, confirm that it will replace the current collection, then tap Replace my collection. Restoring replaces the collection; it does not merge two collections.

The backup file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere you trust. Holohound has no automatic iCloud sync or cloud backup service of its own. Scan photos and Pro purchases are not included in a Collection backup; use Restore Purchases for Pro.

Restoring Holohound Pro

If you subscribe, Holohound Pro is tied to your Apple ID, not to a Holohound account — there isn't one. On a new or reinstalled phone signed in to the same Apple ID, open Profile → See Holohound Pro (or tap the Hound message counter) and tap Restore Purchases at the top of the Holohound Pro screen.

Cancelling Holohound Pro, or asking for a refund

If you subscribe, Holohound Pro renews automatically until you cancel it. To stop the next renewal, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends, in your Apple ID's subscription settings (on iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions); you keep Pro until the period you have paid for ends. Apple handles billing and refunds, so a refund request goes to Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. The full subscription terms are in the Terms of Use.

Report a bug or a wrong card

If a card is identified wrongly, or a set is missing printings, tell us the card and set at support@hypersage.ai. Card data comes from the catalogue for that game — TCGdex for Pokémon, Scryfall for Magic, YGOPRODeck for Yu-Gi-Oh!, Lorcast for Lorcana, gcg-api for Gundam — and corrections made there flow back into the app.
