Prices are estimates, not valuations

Prices come from a different feed for each game — TCGplayer via TCGdex for Pokémon, TCGplayer via Scryfall for Magic, YGOPRODeck for Yu-Gi-Oh!, Lorcast for Lorcana, and no feed at all for Gundam, where the app shows no price rather than inventing one. Each is named on the card it prices, and each is a snapshot of a market that moves. They are not an appraisal, not an offer, and not a guarantee that a card can be bought or sold at that figure. Sold-price history and graded prices come from a separate sold-listings feed and are not guaranteed to be available for any card; where they are not connected or do not cover a card, the app says so on the screen rather than substituting a number it does not have.

Graded cards

Only PSA, BGS, CGC and other grading companies can grade a card. Where a card is already graded, the app shows the grade printed on the case and does not second-guess it.

Selling and fees

Where the app calculates what you would net from a sale, those figures use published marketplace fee schedules and exclude postage, packaging, sales tax and any advertising rate you opt into. Holohound does not list, sell, or take custody of your cards, and does not handle your money.

Your content

Your collection is yours. It is stored on your device, we do not claim any rights over it, and you can export it to CSV at any time without paying.
