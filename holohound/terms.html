Prices are estimates, not valuations

Prices come from TCGplayer market data supplied through TCGdex, and they are a snapshot of a market that moves. They are not an appraisal, not an offer, and not a guarantee that a card can be bought or sold at that figure. Where sold-price history and graded prices are not connected, the app says so on the screen rather than substituting a number it does not have.

Graded cards

Only PSA, BGS, CGC and other grading companies can grade a card. Where a card is already graded, the app shows the grade printed on the case and does not second-guess it.
