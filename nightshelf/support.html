Here to help

Support

One developer reads every email. You'll get a real reply, usually within a day or two — support@hypersage.ai.

Do I need a connection?

Not for the library. The included classic books ship inside the app, and the on-device narrator reads them with no network at all — a plane, a basement, or airplane mode are all fine. Only the AI narrators and writing a new Tonight's Story need a connection.

Which books are free?

The free shelf includes The Wind in the Willows, Alice's Adventures in Wonderland, A Christmas Carol, Treasure Island, Black Beauty, Grimm, Aesop, Frankenstein, Journey to the Centre of the Earth, Meditations and Tales from Shakespeare. They stay free for as long as you keep the app, with no account and no ads. Nightshelf Pro opens more of the library, plus the AI narrators, Tonight's Story, and sleep timers past an hour.

The included books are installed either way — that is why the shelf works offline — but a locked book shows a padlock until you subscribe.

Where are the familiar fairy tales?

Open Familiar Tales on Home for familiar selected tales, including Snow White (the historical Snowdrop version), Cinderella and Sleeping Beauty. Their source books remain available too. Search also recognizes familiar alternate titles, including Snowdrop and The Princess and the Pea.

The library offers classic books, AI-written Nightshelf Originals and selected tales. Your installed version shows the available titles and their free or Pro access. Selected tales come from the included classic books, so they are not counted as extra books. They have their own saved place and running time. Familiar Tales stops at the selected tale's ending; Short listens can continue to another eligible tale when continuation is enabled and the timer has time left.

The narrator sounds flat

Check the narrator name in the player. If it shows an iPhone voice, you can choose another installed voice for free. From Nightshelf 1.9.2, whenever your iPhone's voice is reading, Automatic uses the best English voice installed on your iPhone and switches by itself when you download a better one, and you can choose any installed voice in the voice menu, free. For a warmer sound, download one: in iOS Settings, open Accessibility → Spoken Content (Read & Speak on newer iOS) → Voices → English, choose a voice marked Premium or Enhanced, and download it. Larger voices can be over 100 MB, so Wi-Fi helps. With Nightshelf Pro, open the voice picker to see the narrators available in your version. The picker names their provider. From 1.9.11, choose from ten ElevenLabs narrators for English stories, with accent and delivery labels. Automatic and the free English welcome use ElevenLabs Mira. Spanish, French, German, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese stories use matching voices installed on your iPhone. In 1.9.10, Automatic and the welcome use OpenAI Mira; earlier versions use OpenAI Nova. Tap the play button beside a narrator to hear a sample before choosing.

Mira read the first few minutes, then the voice changed

If you don't have Nightshelf Pro and your voice is set to Automatic, the welcome narrator reads your first few minutes of Nightshelf's books once on each iPhone. From 1.9.11 that narrator is ElevenLabs Mira for English books. Version 1.9.10 uses OpenAI Mira; versions 1.9.3 through 1.9.9 use OpenAI Nova. Then, at the end of a paragraph, your iPhone's own voice carries on, offline, for as long as you like. The minutes are counted in text, not time, so a faster reading speed makes them shorter. Every paragraph sent for the welcome narrator to read counts, including the next one, which is fetched early to reduce pauses. Reinstalling or updating Nightshelf doesn't start them again. If you chose a particular iPhone voice, the welcome doesn't read. You can switch to your iPhone's voice sooner from the welcome note in the player, and Settings → AI features → AI narrators for Nightshelf's books turns AI narrators off. Nightshelf Pro lets you choose an AI narrator, subject to connection, availability and usage limits.

The welcome narrator needs a connection. If it can't be reached, your iPhone's voice reads instead, and unspent welcome minutes are kept for later.

The AI narrator stopped

Cloud narration needs a connection and is subject to provider availability and usage limits. These can include a daily allowance shared by your subscription’s devices and service-wide daily or monthly limits. From 1.9.11, an interruption pauses your chosen AI narrator and keeps your place. Choose Retry beside your narrator’s name to try that narrator again, even while playback is paused, or choose Use iPhone voice to continue that story offline. Earlier versions could switch to an iPhone voice and retry the AI narrator automatically.

If the notice says Pro couldn't be confirmed with the narrator, choose Retry beside your narrator’s name. If Pro is no longer active, choose Check Pro. If that keeps happening, use Restore Purchases and see Restoring Nightshelf Pro below.

Why does the narrator sometimes pause?

AI narration is prepared over the internet. In 1.9.10, Nightshelf Pro can prepare up to three paragraphs ahead, with a small text limit on the two extra paragraphs, to reduce waits during short dialogue. The free welcome prepares one paragraph ahead. A slow connection or a delayed voice service can still cause a wait; choosing an installed iPhone voice works offline.

It kept reading after I fell asleep

Set the sleep timer before you start. Narration fades out rather than stopping abruptly, so it does not jolt you awake. Your place in the book is saved either way, so you can pick up wherever you drifted off.

My progress did not follow to my other device

Progress syncs through your own private iCloud storage, so both devices need to be signed into the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive on. There is no Nightshelf account and no server copy of your synced progress. With iCloud off, progress stays on one device. Ambient sound choices and their volume are local to each device.

Which edition am I listening to?

The library includes novels, collections, retellings and selected tales. In the player, open More options → About this book for a title’s format, source and credits. Some editions omit illustrations, editorial notes or other supporting material.

Tonight's Story will not generate

Writing a new story needs a connection. You can start reading when its first chapter is ready. From 1.9.11, its cover illustration can begin while the remaining chapters are written; the player shows when the cover is still being painted. The illustration can take longer than the writing and appears automatically when it is ready. If writing keeps failing, check your connection and try a shorter prompt. If the app says tonight's stories are spent, that is the daily cap — a few fresh stories a day, then it resets. Every book already on your shelf still reads aloud in the meantime.

Restoring Nightshelf Pro

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases. If the Apple Account signed in to the App Store has an active subscription, Nightshelf Pro opens again — there is no account to log into. If the App Store cannot be reached, the app says so rather than telling you there is no subscription; try again once you are back online.

A subscription redeemed with an offer code can use the cloud narrators, Tonight's Story and story covers across your devices once its current Apple receipt is verified. All those devices share the subscription's service limits. There is no fixed five-device refusal. If access still cannot be confirmed, check that the subscription is active under the Apple Account used for Media & Purchases, use Restore Purchases, and tap the narrator banner to retry. If it persists, email us with the message shown; please do not send your receipt or any story details.

Refunds and billing

All purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com, and manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Reporting a bug

Email support@hypersage.ai with what you saw and what you were doing. Your device model and iOS version help a lot.

Where your data goes

The privacy policy has the full details. In short, the app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no third-party tracker and no advertising. Reading progress, your saved shelf and supported reading settings can sync through your private iCloud. Ambient sound choices and their volume stay on this device.

The on-device narrator speaks every word on the phone, so no book text is sent for narration while an iPhone voice you chose is reading. In earlier versions, an iPhone voice could stand in automatically for an interrupted AI narrator while paragraphs ahead were still sent. From 1.9.11, an interruption pauses your chosen AI narrator. You can retry it or choose Use iPhone voice for this listening session. While that local choice stays active, no new narration requests are sent for the story. Retrying, choosing an AI narrator or starting a new session can send its words again. Switching to the iPhone voice cannot take back words already sent, and requests already sent may still finish. During Mira’s free welcome, a temporary interruption can still use your iPhone’s voice and retry the AI narrator. From Nightshelf 1.9.3, AI narrators can read the books that come with Nightshelf without asking first — ElevenLabs Mira for your first few minutes of English books on each iPhone from 1.9.11 (OpenAI Mira in 1.9.10 and OpenAI Nova in earlier versions), or your chosen narrator with Nightshelf Pro — and they are on from the start, with Automatic as the starting voice. The paragraph being read and the next one go to Nightshelf's own server (hosted for us by Fly.io in the United States), along with up to two more short paragraphs for Pro buffering in 1.9.10, and then to ElevenLabs for English narration from 1.9.11. Spanish, French, German, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese stories use matching voices installed on your iPhone. Earlier app versions use OpenAI narration. The selected provider gets that text, the voice choice, language and reading settings, with no Nightshelf device or subscription ID. ElevenLabs stores submitted text and generated audio in our provider account history. Its terms and our account arrangements govern how long that history remains; Nightshelf does not delete each history item after a reading or promise immediate deletion. Our server sees your IP address, as any website does, and keeps a scrambled daily character count to limit use; with Pro, the request also carries the ID and receipt described in the privacy policy. Settings → AI features → AI narrators for Nightshelf's books turns this off. If you said no when Nightshelf asked about its cloud features in an earlier version, on this iPhone or on an iPhone whose backup this one was restored from, the switch starts off, and because it syncs through iCloud, it is then off on your other devices too.

Anything you choose or type is different: Tonight's Story, its cover, and an AI narrator reading a story you made need your permission first. ElevenLabs narration of stories you made asks separately on this iPhone; an earlier OpenAI permission does not authorize it. Settings → AI features → ElevenLabs narration reviews or withdraws that permission without changing your OpenAI permissions. From Nightshelf 1.9.3, the Tonight's Story & your stories setting under Settings → AI features, not the AI narrators switch, turns them off. In Nightshelf 1.9.2 and earlier, every cloud narration asks first, including the books that come with Nightshelf.

Starting with Nightshelf 1.9.6, your picks and detail go through Nightshelf's server to OpenAI to write a new story, after an updated writing permission on this device. An earlier permission does not authorize this change. Declining or dismissing the writing request leaves any earlier permission for covers and narration unchanged; you can manage writing separately in Settings → AI features, or turn off Tonight's Story & your stories to withdraw all three. Earlier app versions continue sending writing requests to Anthropic. A cover's title, tone and setting go to OpenAI to be painted.

Smaller requests also go out without asking, and the privacy policy spells them out. Tapping ▶ to audition a narrator sends one fixed sample sentence — the same one for everyone, built into the app. It carries no page of a book and nothing you have typed, which is why it isn't gated, and it only happens when you tap ▶. Before Nightshelf 1.9.3 it carried the random device or subscription ID described there for every listener; from 1.9.3 it does so only with Nightshelf Pro. That ID is not anonymous: the records it keys can be linked to your device or subscription, even though they hold no name or email. A check at launch asks whether the server is awake when an AI narrator would read; it carries no text. In versions that support narrator discovery, opening the English voice picker also checks Nightshelf’s server for available narrators. That lookup sends no story text, narrator selection, device or subscription ID, or receipt, and creates no audio. The server sees the internet address used to reach it.

Our server keeps a scrambled copy of a random ID so daily limits can be counted per device or subscription. From Nightshelf 1.9.3, a listener without Pro sends no such ID: the welcome and ▶ auditions are counted against a scrambled copy of the IP address instead, and the record of how much of the welcome you've heard stays on your iPhone and is never sent. Each phone has its own ID and keeps it through a reinstall; a phone that picks up a subscription bought in the app switches to the ID saved with that purchase. A subscription redeemed with an offer code has no such ID, so the server keeps a separate record for it — Apple's transaction IDs, the first device's random ID, and a bounded recent-device history of scrambled device IDs and first- and last-seen times — which the privacy policy sets out in full. The server also keeps narration it has already made in a shared cache, filed under a fingerprint of the paragraph rather than under a device or subscription. No Nightshelf account or sign-in is required. Optional story details are sent as entered, so please leave personal information out.
