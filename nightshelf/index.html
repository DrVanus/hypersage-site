Features

Everything for a good night

Simple and unhurried, the way bedtime should feel.

A Shelf for the Night

Classic books, from Grimm's fairy tales to Austen, Verne and ancient philosophy. Start with the free shelf; Nightshelf Pro opens more of the library.

Find a Familiar Tale

Open Snow White, Cinderella, Sleeping Beauty and other familiar tales directly, with their own saved place. These are standalone selections from the classic collections, with running times and notes about each version's tone.

Explore a Wider Library

Discover Frankenstein, Journey to the Centre of the Earth, Meditations and Tales from Shakespeare in the classic library. Shakespeare's stories are Charles and Mary Lamb's prose retellings; the Odyssey is Samuel Butler's prose translation.

Conjure a Story

Pick a place, a mood, and a length, and AI writes you a calm new bedtime story, with a cover painted to match. It's called Tonight's Story, and it's part of Nightshelf Pro.

Read Along, or Listen

The on-device voice reads every book aloud, even in airplane mode. From Nightshelf 1.9.10, Nightshelf Pro adds Mira and Rowan, two warm AI narrators.

Music, Sounds & Sleep Timer

Rain, fireplace, night crickets, soft piano and more play quietly under the voice. The sleep timer fades it all out as you fall asleep, or lets the sound play on after the story ends.

Private by Design

No account, no ads, and no trackers in the app. Your reading progress, saved shelf and supported reading settings can sync through your own iCloud. Ambient sound choices and their volume stay on this device. Tonight's Story asks before anything you choose or type leaves your phone, and the privacy policy shows exactly what the AI narrators send and where it goes.

Right Where You Left Off

Every story remembers your place, so tomorrow's chapter starts exactly where tonight's ended. With iCloud, it follows you to a new phone.

Common questions

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for and no password to remember. Your reading progress, saved shelf and supported reading settings can sync through your own private iCloud. Ambient sound choices and their volume stay on this device. Nightshelf keeps no server copy of your synced progress.

What's free, and what needs Nightshelf Pro?

The free shelf includes classic books, Nightshelf Originals and selected tales. The app labels which choices are free and which are included with Pro. You get the on-device voice, every sound and piece of sleep music, the sleep timer, and screen dimming, with no time limit and no ads. Nightshelf Pro opens more of the library, AI narrators (Mira and Rowan from 1.9.10), 90- and 120-minute sleep timers, and Tonight's Story. Prices are shown in the app, and the annual plan includes a 7-day free trial if your Apple Account is eligible.

Does it work offline?

Yes, the whole shelf does. Every book ships inside the app, and the on-device voice reads any of them with the network off, ambient sound and sleep timer included. Only the AI narrators and Tonight's Story need a connection.

Which editions are included?

The library includes novels, collections, retellings and selected tales. Editions can omit illustrations, notes or other supporting material. In the player, open More options → About this book for a title’s format, source and credits.

Where do my words go?

Your own words go nowhere unless you say so. Reading, listening in your iPhone's voice, ambient sound, and the timer all happen on your phone. Tonight's Story, and an AI narrator reading a story you made, ask your permission before anything you chose or typed leaves; say no and they stay off while everything else keeps working. From Nightshelf 1.9.3, AI narrators can read the books that come with Nightshelf without asking, and they are on from the start. In 1.9.10, Mira reads your first few minutes once on each iPhone, or Mira or Rowan reads with Nightshelf Pro; earlier versions use Nova for the welcome and Automatic. The paragraph being read and a small amount ahead go to Nightshelf's server, which sees your IP address as any website does and keeps a scrambled daily character count to limit use (with Pro, the request also carries the ID and receipt the privacy policy describes), and on to OpenAI, which gets only that text, the narrator's name and a reading-style note, with no ID. Settings → AI features → AI narrators for Nightshelf's books turns that off; versions 1.9.2 and earlier ask before any cloud narration. Auditioning a narrator with ▶ doesn't ask first, because it sends a fixed sample sentence, never anything of yours. The privacy policy names every stop your words make.

When can I get it?

Now. Nightshelf is on the App Store for iPhones running iOS 17 or later. The whole app stays dark, because it's made for the last minutes of the day.
