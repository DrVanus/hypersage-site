Common questions

A few things before you settle in.

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for and no password to remember. Your reading progress, saved shelf and supported reading settings can sync through your own private iCloud. Ambient sound choices and their volume stay on this device. Nightshelf keeps no server copy of your synced progress.

What's free, and what needs Nightshelf Pro?

The free shelf includes classic books and selected tales. The app labels which choices are free and which are included with Pro. You get the on-device voice, every sound and piece of sleep music, the sleep timer, and screen dimming, with no time limit and no ads. Nightshelf Pro opens more of the library, the AI narrators available in your version, 90- and 120-minute sleep timers, and Tonight's Story. Prices are shown in the app, and the annual plan includes a 7-day free trial if your Apple Account is eligible.

Does it work offline?

Books in the bundled library are installed with the app. An installed matching iPhone voice can read them with the network off, with ambient sound and the sleep timer. Cloud narrators and Tonight's Story need a connection. Complete translations already saved on your iPhone can also be read offline with an installed voice.

Which editions are included?

The library includes novels, collections, retellings and selected tales. Editions can omit illustrations, notes or other supporting material. In the player, open More options → About this book for a title’s format, source and credits.

Where do my words go?

Your own words go nowhere unless you say so. Reading, listening in your iPhone's voice, ambient sound, and the timer all happen on your phone. Tonight's Story, and an AI narrator reading a story you made, ask your permission before anything you chose or typed leaves; say no and they stay off while everything else keeps working. From Nightshelf 1.9.3, AI narrators can read the books that come with Nightshelf without asking, and they are on from the start. In current versions (1.9.17 and later), ElevenLabs Clara reads your first few minutes of English books once on each iPhone; with Nightshelf Pro, Automatic uses ElevenLabs Mira or you can choose another available English narrator. Earlier ElevenLabs releases used Mira for the welcome. Version 1.9.10 uses OpenAI Mira for the welcome and Automatic; earlier versions use OpenAI Nova. The paragraph being read and a small amount ahead go to Nightshelf's server, which sees your IP address as any website does and keeps a scrambled daily character count to limit use (with Pro, the request also carries the ID and receipt the privacy policy describes), and on to ElevenLabs from 1.9.11, or OpenAI in earlier versions. The selected provider gets that text, the voice choice, language and reading settings, with no Nightshelf device or subscription ID. ElevenLabs narration of stories you made requires separate permission on this iPhone; an earlier OpenAI permission does not authorize it. Settings → AI features → AI narrators for Nightshelf's books controls narration of the bundled books. Stories you made use separate permissions: review ElevenLabs narration under AI features, or turn off Tonight's Story & your stories there to withdraw permission for writing, covers and narration. Versions 1.9.2 and earlier ask before any cloud narration. Auditioning a narrator with ▶ doesn't ask first, because it sends a fixed sample sentence, never anything of yours. In versions with narrator discovery, opening the English voice picker checks Nightshelf’s server for available choices without sending story text, a device or subscription ID, or a receipt. The privacy policy names every stop your words make.

Can I read a story in another language?

Current versions let you choose the original edition or a complete translation already saved on this iPhone. When saved editions are available, open More options → Reading language in the story. New translation preparation is no longer offered. Saved text is available offline; listening starts with a matching installed iPhone voice. With Pro, an available AI narrator can read an English translation after separate permission. The original edition and saved place stay available. Translations can contain mistakes. See translation help.

When can I get it?

Now. Nightshelf is on the App Store for iPhones running iOS 17 or later. The whole app stays dark, because it's made for the last minutes of the day.
