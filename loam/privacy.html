Privacy Policy

Loam, by HyperSage AI Labs LLC · Updated November 24, 2026

Preparing for launch. Loam is not yet available on the App Store. This policy describes the upcoming release, including optional Loam Plus. Your notebook is stored on your device; AI sharing needs a separate choice. Purchases, dictation, backups and support messages have their own data paths below.

Your notebook on your device

Notes, checklists, sketches, transcripts, chat history, Ledger entries, reminders and settings are stored locally. The iPhone app keeps a private file snapshot and a WebView recovery cache. Local files may be included in system device backups. If a file cannot be read, Loam may preserve the damaged bytes privately before replacing them; this is a recovery precaution, not a trash folder or guaranteed recovery. Loam does not provide cloud notebook sync.

Optional AI sharing

Before the first AI request, Loam names Anthropic and explains what each feature sends through Loam's service to Claude. Declining keeps local writing, editing, search and export available. After permission:

  • Chat: your question, note titles and collection names, relevant note text, and up to 12 recent conversation turns are sent. Each previous turn is capped at 1,500 Unicode code points. If you attach a photo, it is sent for the requested analysis; a thumbnail may remain in local chat.
  • Look deeper: the selected notes, or up to three recent notes, are sent with up to 12 collection names, including names from unrelated collections. Related Ledger/reminder labels and power settings provide context.
  • Basic Tidy: supported spelling, shorthand and spacing corrections use local rules rather than a remote AI request.

In Powers → Companion consent, tap Withdraw, then Not now — just notes. Pending app requests are cancelled and new AI work stops. Withdrawal cannot recall a request a provider already received. Pause Loam suspends assistance without withdrawing the saved permission. Buying Plus does not grant AI permission.

Usage and subscription verification

AI requests carry a random per-install device identifier for free usage limits and abuse prevention. Optional Plus requests also carry Apple-signed transaction proof and a pseudonymous purchase-account token. These verify the product and current entitlement. Apple handles payment details; Loam does not receive your card information. The purchase token is kept in synchronizable Keychain and may sync through Apple's Keychain service, separately from your notes.

Loam's service keeps monthly or trial allowance records under hashed device or subscription identifiers. Each record contains used or reserved request counts, a provider-cost reservation estimate and the period expiry. To prevent duplicate charges when a request is retried, it also keeps a hashed request identifier, a one-way request-body fingerprint, the allowance identifier, request status and timestamps. These records contain no note, photo or reply text. Allowance and duplicate-request records are pruned when a later request is processed after their period has expired for more than 35 days. Aggregate monthly cost records contain no per-user content.

Apple-signed subscription notifications update a separate record containing a hashed transaction identifier, event date, expiry and revoked status. Expired status records are pruned on notification delivery after expiry plus three days; active or refunded-period records may remain until that expiry. All pruning is opportunistic rather than an exact deletion deadline.

The service does not deliberately store raw purchase proofs, account tokens or raw transaction identifiers in these records. It does not maintain a database of note or photo content, and its application logs exclude those request bodies. Content needed for an AI response goes to Anthropic; the device identifier and purchase proof do not. Device and purchase identifiers are pseudonymous, not anonymous. Notes and photos may also identify you, and Loam does not anonymize their contents before the processing you request.

Anthropic and hosting

Anthropic's commercial API policy describes standard input/output retention of up to 30 days, with exceptions including safety enforcement, legal requirements, particular services/models and separate agreements. Commercial inputs and outputs are excluded from model training by default, with opt-in and feedback exceptions. Loam does not train its own models on your notes. See Anthropic's retention policy and training policy. These terms do not provide an unconditional deletion deadline.

Loam's relay is hosted by Fly.io. Hosting and AI processing use service providers to operate the requested features. Loam does not sell your data or share it for advertising. There is no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDK in the app.

Apple services and sharing

Dictation separately asks for microphone and Apple Speech permission. Apple may process audio on-device or on its servers. Loam's service receives no dictation audio; transcribed text may later be included in an AI request you permit.

Accepted reminders use local notifications with system permission. A photo you select is shared with AI only if you attach it to a permitted request. Exports and the share sheet send copies to the destination you choose. The system rating prompt keeps its timing record locally; it does not send your rating history to Loam.

Support and response reports

Report reply lets you enter a reason and choose whether to include the reply text. The text is excluded by default. Loam prepares an email or copies a report; you send it through your own mail app. No report is sent automatically and no notes, photos or purchase proof are attached. Your email provider supplies the sender address and any content you choose to add.

If you email support or request a launch notice, we receive that correspondence and your email address. We use it to respond, investigate the issue or send the notice you requested. We keep correspondence while needed to handle the request, maintain necessary support records or meet legal obligations. Ask us to stop launch notices or delete support correspondence at support@hypersage.ai; legal or security recordkeeping may limit deletion.

Deletion and separate copies

Deleting a note removes it from Loam's active notebook and current saved state, and redacts related Ledger and chat traces. Loam has no built-in trash or undelete command. Earlier exports, shared copies, clipboard content, system backups, private unreadable-file recovery copies and requests already received by providers are separate. Local deletion does not erase them. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription; use your Apple subscription settings.

In Powers, Back up all notes exports notes, sketches and collections; chats and reminders are not included. Copy all notes as Markdown copies your notebook to the clipboard. Keep exported backups somewhere you control.

Adult audience and changes

Loam is intended for adults aged 18 or older, not for children or teenagers under 18. The AI permission step asks you to affirm that you are at least 18; it does not verify your identity or collect your date of birth. There is no Loam sign-in profile, but optional AI requests may contain personal information. Contact us if you believe a minor has supplied information that needs attention.

This policy will be updated when data practices change. Material changes to AI sharing will be explained before renewed permission is requested.
