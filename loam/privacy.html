Privacy Policy

Loam, by HyperSage AI Labs LLC · Last updated September 12, 2026

The short version. Your notes live on your device. There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics. Content leaves your device only when the AI companion is working for you — and only after you've said yes to waking it. Deleting a note really deletes it.

What Loam stores, and where

Everything you make in Loam — notes, checklists, sketches, voice-note transcripts, chat history, the Ledger of companion actions, reminders, and your settings — is stored locally in the app's storage on your device. Loam has no accounts and no sign-in; we cannot read your notes, and we keep no copy of them.

What leaves your device, and when

Loam sends content off your device only after you consent to waking the companion — it asks before its first send, names its AI provider, and works fully as a private notes app if you decline. After consent, content is sent only while the companion is doing something you asked for or dialed in:

  • Asking your pile a question (chat). Your message, your note titles, the handful of notes most relevant to your question, and the recent messages of that conversation (so Loam doesn't ask you to repeat yourself) are sent to Loam's server, which forwards them to Anthropic's Claude API and returns the answer. If you attach a photo in chat, it is sent for reading; a small thumbnail stays in your chat on the device, and we don't store the image.
  • A deeper tending. When you ask Loam to look deeper at notes, those notes (trimmed) travel with light context: your collection names, recent Ledger entry labels, reminder labels, and your power-dial settings — so proposals fit how you've set Loam up.
  • Basic tidying stays on the device. The Tidy pass that fixes typos and shapes rambly jots is deterministic code running locally — no AI service ever touches your words for it.

Requests carry a pseudonymous device identifier (a random ID minted on your device) used only to meter daily usage and prevent abuse. It is not tied to your name, email, or any account, because none exist.

Changing your mind

You can withdraw consent at any time. In Loam, open Powers and tap Withdraw on the Companion consent row: the companion goes back to sleep and nothing more is sent. Pause Loam, on the row below, suspends the companion without withdrawing consent. Either way, your notes, capture, history, undo and redo keep working exactly as before.

Our server keeps no content

Loam's server is a thin relay. It forwards your request to Anthropic and returns the reply. It has no database of notes, messages, or photos, and it does not log their contents. The one thing it does keep is a count of requests per device identifier, per day — a number, not your content — stored on its own disk and deleted after a few days, so that the daily allowance holds across server restarts.

Anthropic (the AI provider)

The companion runs on Claude, by Anthropic. Content sent to the Claude API is retained by Anthropic for up to 30 days for trust-and-safety purposes and is not used to train models. Neither we nor Anthropic use your notes for training — no exceptions.

Dictation

Voice capture uses Apple's speech recognition. Your audio is processed by Apple on your device or Apple's servers according to your iOS settings; it is never sent to Loam's server. Only the transcribed text lands in your note, on your device.

Reminders and notifications

Reminders you accept are scheduled as local notifications on your device, and iOS asks your permission before the first one is scheduled. A reminder's label travels only when you ask Loam to look deeper at the note it belongs to, as described above; the notification itself never leaves your device.

What we never do

  • No accounts, and no collection of your name, email, or contacts.
  • No advertising, and no advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs. The only record of use we keep is the per-device request count described above, and only to enforce the daily allowance.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No training AI models on your notes.

Deletion — and why there's no trash can

Deleted means deleted. When you delete a note, it is removed from your device, its Ledger entries are redacted, and its traces are scrubbed from chat history — there is no trash folder and no thirty-day holding period, which is why Loam asks you to confirm. Our server holds none of your content, so there is nothing of yours to delete there; its per-device request counts expire on their own within a few days. If you want everything gone, delete your notes in the app (or delete the app itself — all Loam data lives inside it).

Export

Your pile is yours, anytime: in Powers, Back up everything saves one file with every note, and Copy all notes as Markdown puts your whole pile on the clipboard to paste wherever you like.

Children

Loam is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no sign-up, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If Loam's data practices change, this policy will change in the same release, with the date above updated. Material changes will be called out in the app.
