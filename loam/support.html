How do I undo something Loam did?

Open the Ledger tab. Every companion action is a row with its own Undo; today's rows also have an "Undo all" that rolls them back as one. Inside a tidied note, the banner offers Undo and "View original" directly. Undo is surgical — undoing an old tidy never clobbers a newer filing. One honest caveat: if you've written into a note the companion captured for you, undoing that capture is refused so your newer words are never eaten — delete the note itself if you really mean to.

I deleted a note. Can you recover it?

No — and that's by design. Loam has no trash can and keeps no server copies, so a confirmed delete is final everywhere: the note, its Ledger entries, and its chat traces. The app makes you confirm precisely because there's no net.

How do I get my notes out?

Powers → Export notes (.md) exports your whole pile as Markdown. Any single note can be shared as text from its own menu (⋯ → Share a copy, or long-press its card in the pile).

The companion stopped acting on its own

Check three dials-and-doors in Powers: the power in question may be set to Off or Ask first; Loam may be paused; or consent may be withdrawn (the Companion row shows Awake/Asleep). On the free tier the companion also rests when the daily allowance is used — your notes, capture, and undo keep working regardless.

A reminder didn't ping

Reminders use iOS notifications: check Settings → Notifications → Loam is allowed. Loam schedules a ping only when your reminder names a moment it can read ("by Friday", "tomorrow", "tonight"); anything else stays on the reminders shelf without a ping.

Dictation isn't hearing me

Dictation uses Apple's speech recognition — iOS will have asked for microphone and speech permissions on first use; re-enable them in Settings → Privacy if declined. If the mic cuts out mid-dictation, the words captured so far stay on the page.
