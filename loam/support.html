Support

Loam, by HyperSage AI Labs LLC · November 24, 2026

Preparing for launch. Loam is not yet available on the App Store. These instructions describe the upcoming release. For help, email support@hypersage.ai. Explain what happened; remove private content from screenshots first.

Can I use Loam without AI?

Yes. Choose Not now — just notes during setup. Local writing, editing, search and export remain available. To enable AI later, open Powers → Companion consent and review the named-provider permission. Buying Plus does not enable AI automatically.

How do I undo or redo a change?

Supported confirmations and Ledger rows offer Undo; supported undone rows offer Redo. An old tidy can be undone while preserving a newer filing. Inside a tidied note, use Undo tidy or View original. Later edits can retire a row or block redo. Undoing a capture deletes its note, and captures and reminders cannot be redone after undo. The Ledger explains the limits; supported undo and redo do not spend the daily allowance.

Can I recover a deleted note?

Loam has no trash folder or built-in undelete command. If you exported a backup before deletion, you can restore from that file. Deletion removes the note from the active notebook and redacts related Ledger/chat traces. System backups and copies saved elsewhere are separate; prior provider requests follow the privacy policy. Recovery of an unreadable local file is not a promise to recover a deleted note.

How do I export or back up my notes?

In Powers, Back up all notes saves your notes, sketches and collections in one file. Chats and reminders are not in that export. Copy all notes as Markdown copies your notebook to the clipboard. A note's menu also offers Share a copy. Keep backups somewhere you control.

What are Free and Loam Plus?

The upcoming release includes five companion actions per device per local day for Free. Optional Loam Plus provides 100, with monthly or annual billing. Approved proposals, accepted chat actions and a successful Look deeper pass use actions. Chat answers do not consume this counter; online AI requests have separate allowances: 10 per month for Free and 120 per monthly allowance period for Plus, including annual plans. An eligible 14-day trial includes 30 AI requests in total. The app shows the next reset date. Capture, editing, search, export and supported undo/redo are not metered.

Subscriptions are being prepared and are not yet on sale. When available, the Loam Plus card in Powers will show the localized full prices and any eligible offer before purchase. Both billing periods provide the same features.

A completed AI request uses one online request. Explicit refusals and failed responses return that request credit. The app shows when your allowance resets. Paid requests use your subscriber allowance independently of the Free service pool. If a temporary service problem occurs, keep using local notes and try again later.

Who is Loam for?

Loam is intended for adults aged 18 or older. The AI permission step asks for your adult self-affirmation without collecting a date of birth. It is separate from permission to share note context with Anthropic. See the terms and privacy policy.

Restore, manage or cancel a subscription

In the upcoming app, open Powers → Loam Plus → Restore Purchases, using the Apple account that made the purchase. Manage Subscription opens Apple's management screen. You can also use iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting Loam does not cancel a subscription. If a purchase is pending, wait for Apple's approval; do not buy repeatedly. If products or the service are unavailable, close the sheet and keep using your local notebook.

Why has the assistant stopped?

Check Powers: a power may be Off or Ask first, Loam may be paused, or AI permission may be withdrawn. The local action allowance or online service limit may also be reached. Writing, search, export and supported undo/redo remain available.

A reminder did not notify me

Check Settings → Notifications → Loam. A notification requires permission and a scheduled time Loam can interpret. A reminder without a scheduled time remains in the app without a notification.

Dictation is not hearing me

Dictation requires microphone and Apple Speech permissions. Check the app's permissions in iPhone Settings if declined. Apple's recognition availability can also depend on language and connectivity.

Report an AI reply or a bug

Use Report reply under an assistant message. Enter a reason and optionally include the reply; the reply text is excluded by default. Loam prepares an email or copies the report for support@hypersage.ai. You choose what to send in your mail app; nothing is sent automatically. Please omit unnecessary personal information.

For other issues, email support with the app version, what you expected and what happened. AI can make mistakes; check important answers against your notes.

Privacy questions

The privacy policy covers AI, purchases, local storage, backups and user-directed support messages.
