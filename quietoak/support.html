Every email is read by a person, not a bot. You'll get a real reply, usually within a day or two.

Quick help

💬Companion not replying?

Replies are generated live. If a reply fails, check your connection and use the retry control. Do not delete the app as a troubleshooting step: that can remove local data. The written diary, skills, chains, plans and bundled crisis resources remain available without AI.

⭐Restore Quietoak Plus?

In the iPhone app, open the Plus screen and tap Restore Purchases. If your Apple ID has an active subscription, it re-links in seconds.

🪶Hit the free chat limit?

Companion chat includes 5 messages a day on the free tier, resetting each day; Quietoak Plus raises that to 100. A message with a photo attached counts as three, because the image goes through a separate safety check first. The Crisis Survival Kit has no message allowance and stays free, as do the skills library and written diary. Chat is not an emergency service.

🎙️Voice dictation isn't working?

Diary dictation needs microphone permission: iOS Settings → Quietoak → Microphone. The audio is sent to our backend and on to OpenAI only to produce the text, then discarded — it isn't stored.

🔒AI paused after deleting data?

Open Me to review the deletion status. Remote AI features stay paused until local removal and server deletion are both confirmed. Retry server deletion when you are connected. If local removal failed, the app explains what remains; a newly confirmed full deletion also includes data created since your earlier request. Local DBT tools remain available.

🐛Found a bug?

Email support@hypersage.ai with what you saw and what you were doing. Screenshots help — please avoid including anything private from your diary or chat.

💸Refund or billing?

Purchases in the iPhone app are handled by Apple. If you subscribe, request refunds via reportaproblem.apple.com, and manage or cancel Plus in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

For clinicians, reviewers & press

If you're a DBT-informed clinician, a reviewer, or a journalist evaluating Quietoak, reach out at support@hypersage.ai. Quietoak is a self-help adjunct based on the DBT (Dialectical Behavior Therapy) model, not therapy. It includes an always-free crisis kit and an independent crisis-safety layer.

Download the app screenshots and brand images from our press kit. Read the published self-harm safety protocol for details on the AI-companion safeguards and crisis-referral process.

Where your data goes

The full data flow is in our privacy policy. In short, your diary and practice are stored on your device, and we keep no copy of your diary on our servers. There's no account and no login. We don't run ads or third-party trackers on your diary or chat. To generate replies, your messages are sent to our backend and an AI provider — together with a short recap of your last 7 logged diary days, including your urge ratings and the first 120 characters of each day's note, so the reply fits your week. Under that provider's current API terms, those messages aren't used to train their models.
