How it works

One companion, from daily practice to harder days.

Not a bot buried in a menu. The companion you choose and bond with for everyday practice is the same warm presence you can talk to when things get hard.

  1. Choose your companion

    Pick one of six gentle companions, each with its own temperament. Name it, and make it yours.

  2. Practice DBT skills together

    Work through guided exercises like STOP, TIPP, and paced breathing with your companion beside you — and log a one-minute diary card.

  3. They're there on the hard days too

    The same companion is one tap away in chat. And the Crisis Survival Kit — 988, 741741, your local lines — is always free and works offline.

Knows you better

It learns what you're navigating.

Tell your companion as much or as little as you like about what you're navigating. It carries that as context — the words it chooses, what it already knows not to ask you twice — so you never have to start from scratch.

  • Never a label. Quietoak doesn't diagnose, treat, or assess you. Your companion holds what you share as context for how it talks with you, and always points clinical questions back to your clinician.
  • However sure you are. Mark anything you add as just wondering, pretty sure, or diagnosed — all welcome, none required, and nothing is verified or needed to use the app.
  • Yours to control. Optional and editable anytime — change or clear it whenever you like. It's kept on your device and sent along with your messages so the reply can fit you.
Talk it through

Some days, you'd rather just say it out loud.

Tap once and talk — hands-free, like a real conversation. Your companion listens and answers in a warm spoken voice, so on the nights you can't face typing, you don't have to. Switch back to text whenever you want.

  • Hands-free. Tap to talk, then just speak — no typing, no menus.
  • A voice that matches your companion. You'll hear each one's real voice when you choose — and in chat that voice is part of Plus; the on-device voice stays free for everyone.
  • Always your choice. "Type instead" is one tap away, and the crisis kit is never more than a tap from here either.

See how far you've come

The wins you'd otherwise miss.

Recovery isn't a straight line — so Quietoak counts the quiet wins. The urges you rode out, the days you showed up, the skills you reached for: gathered up and shown back to you, gently, never as a scoreboard.

  • Every urge you ride out. Feeling an urge and not acting on it is the skill working — those count here, not just the hard days.
  • A kind streak, never a guilt trip. See the days you checked in; a missed day is never framed as a broken streak.

Understand a hard moment

Find the link you can change.

When something goes the way you didn't want, chain analysis walks you back through it link by link — what sparked it, what you were already carrying, and each thought, feeling and urge in between. Not to relive it. To find the one place a skill could have changed it.

  • The whole chain, in your words. The spark, what made you more vulnerable that day, then each link — a thought, a feeling, a body sensation, an urge, something you did — laid out in order.
  • Ends on a plan, not a post-mortem. Mark the skills that could change a link next time, and what you'd repair. That plan is what your companion carries into your next conversation.
  • Skip anything, keep everything. Every field is optional and nothing is graded. Your chains stay on your device and are yours to reopen, edit or delete.
  • And then your companion writes back. Finish a chain and, if you want it, the companion you chose reads it and answers — in their own voice, about your chain, naming the link you found and the skill you picked. Never automatic, never graded, and it never counts against your daily chat.

Common questions

FAQ

Is Quietoak therapy?

No. Quietoak is a self-help tool based on the DBT (Dialectical Behavior Therapy) model. It is not therapy, not a diagnosis, and not a substitute for professional care. It's designed to work alongside therapy, or while you're learning the skills on your own.

Is the companion a real person?

No — your companion is an AI guide. It says so, it never claims to be a therapist or a person, and it points you toward people and professional help when that's what's needed. It's there to help you practice skills, not to be something you lean on instead of real support.

Can I talk out loud, or switch companions?

Both. You can have a hands-free voice conversation — tap to talk and your companion answers in a warm spoken voice — or stay in text; it's your choice every time. And you can switch between the six companions whenever you like. Your saved talks and what your companion remembers carry over, so you never start from scratch — and that's free. A premium, more natural voice is part of Quietoak Plus.

What if I'm in crisis?

The Crisis Survival Kit is one tap from anywhere in the app, always free, never paywalled, and works offline — 988 (call or text, US), 741741 (text HOME), and your local lines. If you're in immediate danger, call your local emergency number. Quietoak is not an emergency service.

Is my data private?

Your diary and practice are stored on your device, and we keep no diary on our servers. There's no account and no login. We don't run ads or third-party trackers against your diary or chat. To generate replies, your messages are sent to our backend and an AI provider — together with a short recap of your last 7 logged diary days, including your urge ratings and the first 120 characters of each day's note, so the reply fits your week. Your conversations are saved there — tied only to a random device ID, never your name — so your chats carry over between sessions. You can delete them anytime, and the full data flow is in our privacy policy.

What's free, and what's Plus?

The whole app is free to use: the DBT skills library, the diary card and trends, all six companions, your saved conversations, the crisis kit, and daily companion chat (5 messages a day; a message with a photo attached counts as three, because the image goes through a separate safety check first). Quietoak Plus ($9.99/month or $59.99/year) raises that to 100 messages a day, on the smartest version of them — and adds a premium companion voice (the on-device voice stays free for everyone) and a clinician-ready PDF of your diary card. Crisis support is never limited and never behind Plus.

Who is Quietoak for?

Adults (18+) who want to practice DBT skills in a calm, private space — whether you're in therapy and using it as an adjunct, on a waitlist, or learning the skills on your own.

iPad? Mac? Android?

iPhone first. It runs on iPad in iPhone-compatibility mode for now; a native iPad layout, Android, and Mac aren't committed yet.
