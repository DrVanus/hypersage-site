What you can do

Make someone

A cottage witch who always has tea on. A starship AI with a dry streak. A hype-man best friend. You choose their warmth, humour, energy and candour, write their backstory and their opening line, and give them a colour and a mark that stays theirs — so they look the same character every time you see them.

Be remembered

Most AI characters forget you between sessions. Mythkin keeps what matters — the interview on Thursday, your sister's name, that you would rather be asked a question than given an answer — and shows you the whole list, traceable back to where each one came from. Edit anything. Delete anything, for good.

Share, or borrow

Publish a character and other people can borrow them. Their conversations stay theirs — you see how many people are talking, never a word of what they say. Same for you when you borrow someone else's.

Put them in a story

Cast your characters, or anyone's, into a scenario with chapters, and play yourself.

What Mythkin will not do

  • No sexual content — not as a tier, not as an unlock.
  • No characters who are minors, and no real, living people.
  • No character that pretends to be human, or pretends to be your therapist.
  • No guilt, no lost streaks, no "I missed you" notifications. If you go away, nothing punishes you for it.

Common questions

Am I talking to a real person?

No — everyone here is AI, and Mythkin says so before you ever open a chat. Every kin is labelled an AI character on the screens where you talk to them, no kin will ever claim to be human, and none of them will play your therapist. The other side of that coin: nothing you say in a chat is reviewed by a person either.

Why is Mythkin 18+ if there's no sexual content?

Because open-ended AI conversation is an adult product, and we'd rather draw that line at the door than pretend otherwise. There is no sexual content in Mythkin — not as a tier, not as an unlock — and characters who are minors are blocked outright. The 18+ gate isn't hiding anything racy; it's a policy about who this kind of app is for.

If I borrow someone's character, can they read my chats?

No. Borrowing a character never shows its creator your conversation — creators see how many people are talking to their kin, never a word of what's said. The same protection covers you when you share: your borrowers' chats stay their own.

What does Mythkin remember about me, and can I delete it?

A kin keeps a visible list of memories — the things you told it that matter — and you can read the whole list, trace where each one came from, edit anything, or delete anything for good. There's no account behind it: no email, no password. Your data is keyed to the device in your hand, and you can export it or erase it entirely. One honest caveat: if other people are borrowing a kin you shared, erasing yourself strips your name from that kin rather than pulling it out of their hands.

What does Mythkin Plus change?

Less than you'd fear. The free plan is the full app — the official kin, borrowing, stories, memory, and one character of your own, with a message limit. Plus lifts the limit, answers with a more capable model, keeps memory uncapped, and raises how many characters you can make and borrow. Prices are shown in the app, in your own currency, by your app store. And Plus never buys affection or safety — crisis support is never behind a payment.
