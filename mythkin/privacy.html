Privacy

Every claim on this page is derived from what the code actually does. If you find a sentence here that the app does not honour, that is a bug and we want to hear about it.

The short version

  • You do not need an account or an email address to use Mythkin.
  • Your conversations are sent to Anthropic to generate replies. Character artwork, story covers, painted moments and optional chapter illustrations are generated by OpenAI. We ask your explicit permission before either happens.
  • We do not sell your data, and we never use your conversations to target advertising.
  • You can export everything, and you can delete everything, from inside the app.
  • Mythkin is for adults (18+).

Photos you choose to send

Mythkin never reaches into your photo library. It only ever receives a photo you pick yourself, and only in two places:

  • Making a kin from a photo. You choose up to three photographs — with the camera or from your library, you pick either way — and we paint a portrait from them. Our service removes the input photos after the painting succeeds or reaches a terminal failure; a pending retry can retain them until that request resolves. This does not mean OpenAI deletes its copy immediately: provider retention and safety review follow the API policies below. We do not make the input photos public or use them to train our own models. Location and camera details are stripped before anything is sent. A kin made this way stays in your library: it cannot be shared or borrowed.
  • Sending a photo in a chat. The photo is forwarded to the AI so the character can respond to it, and is not stored on our server afterwards.

Before either one leaves your phone, the location, the timestamp and the camera details recorded inside the image file are stripped off, and the image is re-encoded by us. Photos are sent to OpenAI (for painting) and to Anthropic (for a reply in chat) — the same two companies named on the consent screen when you first opened the app.

Who else receives your data

Anthropic — conversation content

To generate a character's reply, we send the character's description, the relevant part of your conversation, the memories that character is using, and — if you filled it in — what you wrote in Settings → About you, including the name you asked to be called. Anthropic’s commercial API terms restrict processing purposes and require confidentiality and security. API inputs and outputs are not used for model training by default, subject to explicit opt-in and feedback exceptions. Its standard retention is up to 30 days, with safety, legal, model/feature and separate-agreement exceptions. See its commercial terms, retention policy and training policy.

OpenAI — character artwork and optional illustrations

OpenAI processes API content under its services agreement and API data controls. API content is excluded from training by default unless sharing is enabled. Retention and safety review depend on the endpoint and applicable settings; deleting content from Mythkin does not erase copies the provider already received.

When artwork is generated for a character you create, the character's appearance description is sent to OpenAI's image API. Photo-based creation also sends the reference photo as described above. A painted chat moment uses a short AI-written scene description, the character's appearance description and the kin's portrait. A story cover uses the story's title, premise and character references.

If you choose Illustrate this chapter, a separate confirmation asks your permission to send OpenAI a short excerpt from that saved AI-written chapter (up to 240 characters), the character's appearance description and the kin's portrait. This image request does not directly include your own chat messages, saved-memory library or About you profile. Declining the illustration keeps the chapter available to read.

Apple — payments

Subscriptions, if you take one, are handled entirely by the app stores. We receive a signed receipt confirming an active subscription. We never see your card.

Fly.io — hosting

Our server and database run on Fly.io infrastructure in the United States.

We ask for your permission during setup before the normal AI features send content to Anthropic or OpenAI. Optional chapter illustration asks for separate permission for that selected excerpt and portrait each time, before starting. If you decline, that illustration is not sent; you can keep reading the chapter. You can review provider sharing in Settings.

What other people can see

This matters most, so it is stated as a rule rather than as a description:

If you publish a character, other people can borrow it and talk to it. They cannot see your conversations with it. You cannot see theirs. A creator sees counts — how many chats, borrows and likes their character has — and never a word of anyone's conversation, and never anyone's memories.

A character kept private is visible only to you. A character shared by link is visible to whoever has the link.

Keeping and deleting

Saved facts do not expire or get automatically pruned. Both plans use the same 10,000-active-fact safety limit; reaching it pauses new additions without deleting existing facts. Replies use a bounded selection of relevant facts and pins, not the whole saved library. User-authored facts, edits and pins are protected from automatic changes. Ordinary learned facts can be corrected or superseded when supported by a live message you sent.

  • You delete a memory — it leaves the saved-memory recall set immediately and is no longer shown on the memory screen. We retain its text as a deleted marker to suppress automatic re-learning of the same wording. You can add it back yourself. The original conversation is separate and can still be used as conversation context until you delete that conversation.
  • You delete a conversation — its messages go with it. Memories a character learned from it stay, deliberately: a fact you chose to keep is yours, and forgetting is done on the memory screen, where you can see exactly what you are forgetting.
  • You delete a character — it disappears from the marketplace and can no longer be borrowed.
  • You delete everything — Settings → Delete everything. This removes your characters, conversations, messages and memories from our servers. Characters other people have borrowed are anonymised rather than yanked out of someone else's ongoing conversation.

What survives "Delete everything", and why

The app shows you this list at the moment you delete, rather than the word "everything". It is short, and each line is there for a reason we can defend:

  • Crisis referral counts — kept, with the link to you removed. California SB 243 requires us to report referral counts for periods that have already passed, and a counter that can be erased cannot do that. No message text was ever stored.
  • Reports you filed — kept, with your identifier removed. A report is evidence about someone else's content and stays open for a human to finish reviewing.
  • Rate-limit counters for the window you are currently in. They hold a request count and nothing else.
  • A record that a Spark purchase was redeemed — kept, with the device identifier removed, so the same store transaction cannot be credited twice.
  • Purchased Sparks you have not spent — kept with the random app-account identifier and device link needed to reach the balance. This access link is not anonymised while a paid balance remains. If you want that balance removed, spend the Sparks first or contact support. Removing the balance does not issue an App Store refund.

Otherwise we keep your data for as long as you use the app. Server logs, which do not contain conversation text, are kept for up to 30 days for debugging.

Your rights

You can export everything we hold about you as a file, from inside the app, at any time, without asking us. Because we do not require an account, that in-app export is also the fastest way to exercise a GDPR or CCPA access request. One exception, stated so the export is not mistaken for a complete archive: memories you have already deleted are not in it. If you want those too, write to us and ask. For anything else, or if the app is not working, see Support.

Children

Mythkin is for adults. We ask you to confirm you are 18 or older before any character is reachable, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or who receives it, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect, not only on this page.
