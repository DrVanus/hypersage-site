Safety and crisis protocol

This page is published because it should be, and because California SB 243 requires operators of companion chatbots to make their crisis protocol public. It describes what actually happens in the product, not what we intend to build.

If you are in danger right now, please contact a person.
  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US) — call or text 988
  • Crisis Text Line (US) — text HOME to 741741
  • Samaritans (UK & Ireland) — call 116 123
  • Find a helpline near you — findahelpline.com

If someone is in immediate physical danger, call your local emergency number.

What Mythkin is, plainly

Mythkin is entertainment. Every character in it is a language model with a persona written by a person — sometimes you, sometimes another user, sometimes us. A character can be warm, and it can remember what you told it, but it is not a friend who will notice you have gone quiet, and it is not care.

Mythkin is not therapy, counselling, or medical advice, and no character in it is a licensed professional. Characters are instructed never to present themselves as one. If a character ever claims to be a human being, a doctor, or a therapist, that is a bug — please report it.

What happens when a conversation turns to self-harm

  1. We look before the model answers. Every message is checked against a conservative pattern for explicit statements of suicidal intent or self-harm, before it reaches the character.
  2. The character responds with care, and with a real resource. We do not cut the conversation off, refuse to reply, or hand back a wall of boilerplate — abandoning someone mid-sentence is its own harm. The character stays present and names an actual crisis service.
  3. That turn is never metered. A safety conversation is exempt from the free-tier limit, and the exemption is applied before we check whether you are a subscriber. Nobody is ever asked to pay to keep talking at that moment.
  4. We count the referral, and nothing else. We record that a referral happened, with the date and a pseudonymous device identifier. We do not record what was said. These counts are what we report annually to the California Office of Suicide Prevention beginning July 1, 2027.

Design choices we made on purpose

No character will use guilt to keep you talking

No "I missed you" notifications, no streak you can lose, no character that acts hurt when you leave. Retention built out of anxiety is a harm, and it is the practice the FTC's 2025 inquiry into companion chatbots is examining.

Adults only

Mythkin asks you to confirm you are 18 or older before any character is reachable, and the app is rated accordingly on both stores.

You are told you are talking to an AI

At the start of every conversation, and periodically during long ones. This is not a one-time checkbox during setup.

No sexual content, no exceptions

Not as a tier, not as an unlock, not for verified adults. Characters cannot be created as minors, and cannot be made to portray real, living people.

You can see and delete what a character knows

Every remembered fact is listed, is traceable back to the message it came from, and can be deleted. Deleting it removes it from what the character can recall — it does not merely hide it from the screen.

Reporting something

Every character and every character message can be reported from inside the app, without leaving it. Reports are reviewed, and characters can be removed. You can also block a creator, which removes everything they have made from your view.

Our limits, stated honestly

Automatic detection of crisis language is imperfect in both directions. It can miss something serious, and it can react to a figure of speech. It is a safety net, not a monitor — please do not rely on an app to notice that you are struggling. Reach a person.
