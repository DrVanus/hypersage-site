Common questions

A character forgot something important

Open the character's memory screen (the ✎ next to their name). Everything they know is listed there, and you can add a fact yourself. If something is wrong, edit or delete it — deleting really removes it. If a fact you added is still being ignored, pin it: pinned facts are always kept in view.

A character said something it should not have

Report it from the message itself — tap and hold, then Report. Reports are reviewed and characters can be removed. If a character claimed to be a human being, a therapist, or a doctor, please report that specifically; it is a bug and we want to fix it.

Someone published a character that breaks the rules

Report the character from its page, and block the creator if you would rather not see anything else they make. Blocking removes all of their characters from your feed immediately.

How do I cancel Mythkin Plus?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, so they are cancelled there, not by us. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Mythkin → Cancel. On Android: Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by the store under its own policy.

I hit my message limit

The free tier gives you a set number of messages in a rolling window; the app shows exactly how many and when it resets. Conversations about self-harm are never metered. Mythkin Plus removes the limit.

How do I get my data, or delete everything?

Settings → Export my data downloads everything we hold. Settings → Delete everything removes your characters, conversations and memories from our servers. Both work without contacting us. See Privacy.

Can I make a character of a real person?

No — see Terms. Mythology, folklore and public-domain literature are fine, and there are a lot of them.

Will there be adult content?

No. Not as a tier, not as an unlock, not for verified adults.
