Boilerplate

HyperSage AI Labs is an independent studio that makes consumer apps and original games. Its apps cover crypto markets, relationships, health, recovery, food, stories, and sleep. Where an app uses AI, it has a specific job, such as explaining why a portfolio moved, adapting a recipe to what's in the kitchen, or preparing a brief for a doctor's visit. The studio designs, builds, and runs all of it in-house.

The products, one line each

  • Wingmate — four AI dating coaches that remember the people you're talking to and turn a screenshot of your chat into replies you can make your own. Site ↗
  • Quietoak — a self-help DBT skills app. Talk through hard moments with an AI companion that remembers you, practice the skills, and keep a daily diary card. Site ↗
  • CryptoSage AI — a crypto portfolio tracker with an AI that knows what you hold, where you're exposed, and what moved your portfolio. Site ↗
  • Saffra — a personal AI chef whose recipes arrive complete, then adapt to your pantry, allergies, and the time you've got. Site ↗
  • StoryVault AI — branching stories written with AI. Step into Beowulf or Dracula, or start a world of your own from a single line. Site ↗
  • Nightshelf — a bedtime-story app that reads complete classics aloud, with ambient sound underneath and a sleep timer that fades everything out. Site ↗
  • Hexhunter — a gothic horde-survivor with nine heroes. Pick a hero, draft upgrades as you level up, and survive three dark biomes. Site ↗
  • Mythwright — a tactical card battler where the heroes and monsters of classic literature duel across three lanes. Site ↗
  • Rowan — an AI symptom tracker for chronic conditions. Describe your day in plain words, and Rowan turns it into symptom logs and a brief for your next appointment. Site ↗
  • Alder — a private AI companion for recovery. It keeps your sober count, helps with cravings, and walks you through a slip without erasing what you've built. Site ↗
  • Waddleton — a snowy social world in the browser, free to play. Make a penguin, meet other players, and dive into 17 arcade games. Play ↗
  • Mythkin — an AI character app for adults. Talk to 470 figures from history, myth, literature and scripture, or write your own. Site ↗
  • Hoardling — hoard defense for the browser, played from the dragon's side of the cave. Hold the chokepoints and kill a carrier to send its coins home. Site ↗
  • Gemburrow — a cozy gem-digging puzzle in the browser, where every gem and rock is real physics. Pull one out and the whole pile settles. Site ↗
  • Holohound — a trading-card binder for Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Lorcana and Gundam. Scanning is free and unlimited for Pokémon and Magic, the prices it shows name their source, and Hound is an AI that knows your binder. Site ↗
  • Loam — a notes companion that asks before it acts. It tidies your slips in place and writes each change into a Ledger, where you can undo it. Site ↗

CryptoSage AI provides market information, not investment advice. Rowan, Quietoak and Alder support self-care and do not provide medical or mental-health treatment.
