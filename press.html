Boilerplate

HyperSage AI Labs is an independent product studio building distinctive consumer apps for relationships, health, recovery, food, stories, markets, and sleep — alongside original games. Where AI belongs, its products add a product-specific intelligence layer that combines information a person provides and relevant in-product context with domain knowledge, tools, and safeguards. The studio designs, builds, and operates the entire portfolio in-house.
